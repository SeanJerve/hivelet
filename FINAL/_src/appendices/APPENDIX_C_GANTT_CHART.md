# APPENDIX C: GANTT CHART

Figure C-1 shows the schedule as it ran, taken from the project's version history (Appendix M, Table M-19). Each
iteration passed through the phases of the Agile cycle of Section 3.2. The dashed block is the
preparation and submission of the Module 01 documents, between the first build and the first
iteration. The first build ran from 24 July to 28 August 2026, Iteration 1 from 12 September to
31 October, and Iteration 2 from 1 November. Iteration 1 began by checking the system and its
documents against each other, from 12 to 14 September, before the system was put online and tested. Four further technical evaluators reviewed a
demonstration copy of the system from 15 to 18 October. On the redesigned system, the second round
of users ran from 31 October to 10 November, and three technical evaluators reviewed it on
12 November. The interviews and the first design, made for the proposal before July 2026, are
outside the chart.
