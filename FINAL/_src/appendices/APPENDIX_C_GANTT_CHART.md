# APPENDIX C: GANTT CHART

Figure C-1 shows the schedule as it ran, taken from the project's version history (Appendix M, Table M-19). Each
iteration passed through the phases of the Agile cycle of Section 3.2, except that the first had no
deployment work, since the system ran as before. The dashed block is the preparation and submission
of the Module 01 documents, between the first build and the first iteration. The first build ran from
24 July to 28 August 2026, Iteration 1 from 12 to 14 September, Iteration 2 from 15 September to
31 October, and Iteration 3 from 1 November. Four further technical evaluators reviewed a
demonstration copy of the system from 15 to 18 October. On the redesigned system, the second round
of users ran from 31 October to 10 November, and three technical evaluators reviewed it on
12 November. The interviews and the first design, made for the proposal before July 2026, are
outside the chart.
