# Sustainability claims

Status: `draft`  
Intervention: `external-verification`  
Sources: `SRC-SUSTAINABILITY-CLAIMS`, `SRC-SOURCE-EVIDENCE`, `SRC-PROFESSIONAL-SCOPE`, `SRC-PACKAGE-LOCAL-SYNTHESIS`

## Load when

Load when words, numbers, comparisons, seals, certifications, icons, colour,
imagery, material simulation or the whole design can reasonably imply an
environmental property, benefit, reduction or superiority. Include product,
service, organisation, process, package, event and campaign claims. Do not load
for nature imagery that has no plausible environmental meaning in its actual
context. A current jurisdiction, provider or contract modifies an activated
claim review; it never selects this route by itself.

## Inputs, authority and formal variables

Register the exact express wording and each material implied visual message,
including headline, qualifier, seal, icon, image, colour, hierarchy, adjacency
and omission. Record claimant and accountable claim owner; subject and product
variant; audience and likely interpretation; medium and placement; geography;
valid date; lifecycle stages included and excluded; functional unit and
denominator; comparison baseline, comparator and baseline date; measurement or
calculation method; uncertainty; evidence source/version/date; significant
tradeoffs and omitted impacts; certification scheme and relationship; required
qualification; jurisdiction and application date; approval owner; publication
date; expiry, update trigger and dependent outputs.

Separate a measured fact, comparative claim, future target, aspiration,
certification, offset/compensation claim and broad environmental impression.
Do not let one record silently support another category.

## Generate or decide

1. Start from the narrowest material fact the current evidence supports. Name
   subject, scope, lifecycle boundary, baseline, denominator, geography and date
   close enough to the claim to change the whole impression.
2. Compose words and visuals as one claim. Test whether a reasonable viewer
   could infer product-wide, lifecycle-wide, certified, zero-impact or superior
   performance from prominent colour, landscape, leaf/recycling mark, texture,
   seal shape, imagery or hierarchy even when small print is technically true.
3. Make comparisons like-for-like. State what changed, against what, across
   which units and time, with the same relevant method and boundary. Do not
   hide a smaller denominator or shifted lifecycle stage.
4. Make material tradeoffs and exclusions visible at the decision point. A
   qualification must be specific, readable, accessible, timely and consistent
   with the dominant claim; it cannot reverse an unqualified headline.
5. Use certification marks or environmental labels only when the exact subject,
   scheme, version, permission, conditions and current status match. A
   self-created badge must not imitate independent verification.
6. Set expiry and recheck before release. If evidence, product, method, baseline,
   jurisdiction or visual context changes, reopen the claim and all derivatives.

## Critique: failure signatures and causes

- **Broad green halo around a narrow fact:** “green”, “sustainable”, “clean”,
  “climate-friendly” or nature-coded art implies general benefit; scope was
  omitted or relegated to a footnote.
- **Whole impression exceeds wording:** forest imagery, recycled-paper texture,
  seal geometry, dominant green or a large number implies certification or
  superiority; visual implication was never registered as a claim.
- **Percentage without denominator:** a reduction or recycled-content number
  omits unit, comparator, baseline date or included material; a favourable
  denominator was selected after the fact.
- **Lifecycle shifting:** one stage improves while upstream, use, durability or
  end-of-life effects disappear; the boundary was chosen to produce the claim.
- **Insignificant benefit is visually dominant:** a minor or legally required
  change carries product-wide emphasis; materiality was not assessed.
- **Tradeoff or uncertainty disappears:** precise graphics imply certainty or
  net benefit while method, range or contrary impact is material.
- **False certification cue:** an invented leaf/checkmark badge resembles a
  third-party seal, or a real mark is applied beyond its subject/version.
- **Stale claim:** product, source, law, application date, baseline or scheme has
  changed; no owner, expiry or dependency review existed.

Localise exact text and visual evidence, state the plausible impression, the
unsupported scope or relation, authority, cause as confirmed/inferred/unknown,
and release consequence. Do not diagnose compliance from appearance.

## Smallest repair and regression

Narrow, qualify, reframe or remove the unsupported textual and visual claim.
Expose subject, lifecycle boundary, baseline, denominator, date and material
tradeoff; reduce misleading prominence; replace an unverified seal; or withhold
release until current evidence and authority exist. A footnote alone is not a
repair when headline, image, colour or hierarchy still makes the broader claim.

Preserve verified facts, useful product distinction, brand recognition,
legibility and existing evidence history. Recheck headline, body, image, colour,
icon, chart, caption, alt text, packaging face, small/narrow state, social crop,
translation and sales derivative as one impression. Update every dependent
claim and keep prior evidence/correction receipts.

## Rules and exceptions

Binding constraints are exact current evidence, declared claim scope, owner,
qualification, expiry, approved mark terms and applicable current jurisdiction.
Standards and official guidance apply only in their subject, edition,
jurisdiction and date. No fixed palette, prohibited-word list or amount of
small print guarantees an acceptable claim.

An intentionally aspirational, comparative, self-declared or campaign-level
statement is permissible only when its status, measurable target, owner,
boundary, method, timeframe, dependency, uncertainty and non-achievement risk
are clear. A scoped exception never proves the result, compliance or universal
acceptability.

## Proof, ownership and claim ceiling

Bind the final exact words and visual implication to source/version, inspected
scope, calculation, product/version, baseline, geography, owner, approval date
and expiry. Recalculate material numbers; inspect the claim at intended size and
in every format; verify certification state and qualifications immediately
before publication. Record supporting, qualifying and undermining evidence plus
unresolved status.

This module owns environmental claim framing, whole-impression analysis,
lifecycle boundary, qualification and expiry. Evidence owners validate sources;
visual owners control colour and image implications; domain and legal owners
approve release. Report only the tested artifact, evidence and jurisdiction.
Never claim environmental truth, lifecycle completeness, certification,
compliance or absence of tradeoffs from visual review alone.
