# Source verification and evidence

Status: `draft`  
Intervention: `external-verification`  
Sources: `SRC-SOURCE-EVIDENCE`, `SRC-PROFESSIONAL-SCOPE`, `SRC-PACKAGE-LOCAL-SYNTHESIS`

## Load when

Load when a material fact, quotation, number, causal implication, current
standard, citation or source-dependent design decision needs inspection,
independence, currency, correction or downstream reconciliation. Do not load for
a supplied, non-consequential fact the brief explicitly treats as authoritative
unless its evidence state can change the work. Permission and attribution are
not established by this route.

## Inputs, authority and formal variables

Freeze exact proposed wording, speaker, artifact/location, audience, reference
time, geography/population/units, consequence and decision owner. Split compound
statements into atomic, checkable claim IDs. Classify each as empirical, quote,
definition, causal inference, prediction, opinion, implied claim or
authority-owned conclusion; record whether it is checkable, authority-required
or not checkable.

For every source assign a stable ID and record creator/publisher, title, final
URL or persistent identifier, publication/effective/version dates, retrieval
time, exact inspected scope and locator, access state, origin role, shared
origin/dependency, conflicts or relevant interests, lawful receipt/hash when
useful, correction/retraction/supersession state and next recheck. A snippet,
abstract, metadata record, archive or inaccessible source must be labelled as
such; never record it as full inspection.

For each claim-source pair record exactly one relation: `supports`,
`contradicts`, `qualifies`, `context only`, or `does not address`. Keep
supporting and undermining evidence visible. Register every caption, chart,
credit, manifest, translation and output that depends on the claim.

## Generate or decide

1. Verify the exact claim, not a nearby paraphrase. Preserve supplied wording
   until an authorised repair is accepted.
2. Inspect the strongest relevant source to the level the claim requires.
   Prefer original data, rule, statement or record when it reduces transmission
   loss, but treat `primary` as a role rather than an automatic credibility
   score.
3. Trace mirrors, syndication, press releases, shared datasets and citation
   loops to origin. Independence is an origin property, not a URL count.
4. Seek material counterevidence, qualifications, corrected versions and
   superseding authority in proportion to volatility and consequence. No fixed
   source count or universal confidence threshold replaces this judgment.
5. Reconcile time precisely: observed, valid, effective, publication,
   retrieval, correction and review dates are separate. A live link does not
   prove that its content or applicability is current.
6. Assign an evidence state such as `supported for stated scope`,
   `contradicted`, `mixed/disputed`, `outdated`, `unsupported`, `inaccessible`
   or `not checkable`; include confidence basis, limitation and what would
   change the state.
7. Write publication-safe wording that matches the supported scope and
   uncertainty. Attribute authority-owned conclusions instead of converting
   them into model fact.

## Critique: failure signatures and causes

- **Real but irrelevant citation:** source exists yet does not entail the atomic
  claim; topic overlap or keyword match replaced semantic inspection.
- **Snippet or memory verification:** wording, scope, version or context was not
  inspected; search display or model recall was treated as evidence.
- **False corroboration:** several URLs repeat one origin or circular citation;
  link count was mistaken for independence.
- **Unsupported becomes false:** search failure or inaccessible evidence is
  reported as contradiction; missing support and affirmative contrary evidence
  were collapsed.
- **Stale authority:** current law, standard, product figure or public role is
  backed by an older or undated version; currency and effective date were not
  checked.
- **Scope laundering:** evidence for one date, place, population, unit or
  association is broadened into a timeless, universal or causal claim.
- **Correction orphan:** source or statistic changed but captions, charts,
  translations, credits and manifests retain the old claim; dependencies were
  absent.
- **Identifier overclaim:** DOI, archive, hash, signature or provenance proves
  identity/association but is described as truth, independence or currentness.

Localise the exact claim and source relation. State observation, likely factual
effect, evidence authority, cause as confirmed/inferred/unknown, severity,
confidence, smallest repair, protected facts and unresolved state.

## Smallest repair and regression

Correct the parent relation first: narrow or attribute the claim, restore
context, replace or add evidence, mark it disputed/unsupported/inaccessible,
recalculate, or remove it. Then update every dependent headline, caption,
graphic, dataset note, credit, manifest, accessibility equivalent and export.
Append correction and recheck events; never silently overwrite the earlier
source, verdict or artifact receipt.

Preserve exact supported facts, quotations, uncertainty, legitimate dissent,
source confidentiality, stable IDs and prior decision history. Reinspect all
dependents and confirm that a wording repair has not altered units, time,
attribution, implication or visual emphasis.

## Rules and exceptions

Do not invent a source, URL, retrieval, quote, locator, version, relation,
independence, verdict or current status. A citation, official domain, persistent
ID, checksum or valid provenance record identifies evidence but cannot by itself
prove truth, completeness, independence, permission or currency.

Confidential or anonymous evidence, an inaccessible original, one authoritative
record or a lawful local receipt can be legitimate when reason, authority,
inspection limit, safe metadata, corroboration need, consequence and review
point are recorded. Protect restricted evidence; never expose it merely to make
the ledger complete.

Operational design rules remain local and usable while external links are
offline. Preserve the source ID and last inspected receipt, mark availability,
and continue stable craft reasoning. If a current law, standard, provider term,
public fact or other volatile claim cannot be reverified, mark it unverified and
narrow or stop the dependent claim; do not substitute model memory or silently
remap the source.

## Proof, ownership and claim ceiling

Validate unique IDs, atomic claims, exact locators, inspected scope, relation,
origin graph, contrary evidence, version/current status, correction history and
dependency coverage. Recalculate where applicable and inspect the final visible
citation and wording in context. Structural checks prove identity, coverage and
declared lineage only; an editor, fact checker or domain authority validates
meaning and publication judgment.

This module owns claim/source registries, evidence relations, currentness,
correction and dependency history. Domain owners decide subject meaning; rights
owners decide permission; production owners prove survival. Report only what was
actually inspected. Never claim exhaustive research, universal truth, absence
of contrary evidence, automatic fact-checking or currentness beyond the recorded
verification.
