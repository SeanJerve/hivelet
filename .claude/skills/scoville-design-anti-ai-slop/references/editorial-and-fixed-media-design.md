## Load when

Load when fixed dimensions, final-size/viewing context, a page/spread/slide/
panel sequence, reusable document or presentation template, folded publication,
binding, non-navigational exhibition/display surface, or another fixed-medium
relationship can change the design. Do not load merely because delivery is PDF,
image or print; for product-package panels, labels, assembled packaging or SKU
systems; for a physical journey, decision-point schedule or sign family; for
export, preflight or provider acceptance of an approved design; for a simple
fixed ratio with no consequential medium question; or for responsive web.

## Fixed-medium record

Capture only open relevant fields; otherwise use the Core minimal record:

`purpose/audience/consequence | exact content/order | artifact class | final
dimensions/orientation/scale | viewing distance/light/device/substrate |
handling/duration/venue | page/spread/slide/panel/fold/surface map |
binding/gutter/trim/bleed/live area | protected crop/content | sequence thesis |
recurring anchors | permitted variation | medium-changed type/image/colour/space
intent | assumption authority | required proof`.

Artifact classes include single fixed canvas, paged or facing-page publication,
folded piece, report/document, presentation/slide deck, reusable template,
non-navigational exhibition/display panel, and fixed digital display. Product-
package panels and SKU systems belong to Packaging. A sign is in scope here only
as an isolated non-navigational fixed graphic; a physical destination/route
system belongs to Wayfinding. Unknown provider, geometry, binding, projection,
substrate, colour or final-size facts remain unknown. A provisional design may
use labelled assumptions when safe; an assumption is not provider approval.

## Generate and decide as a system

Start with a content map or flat plan. Give every page, spread, slide, panel or
surface a communicative job; map hierarchy and reading order before choosing
grids or furniture. Define a sequence thesis such as entry, development,
evidence, pause, transition, climax, recovery and close only where the content requires
those roles. Establish the least complex recurring anchor system that preserves
orientation while allowing subject-specific variation. Pacing follows content,
audience, viewing duration, page turns, physical handling and visual change - not
a prescribed alternation, template count, hero cadence or uniform density.

Inspect four scopes: detail; page/slide/panel; facing spread/fold/surface
relation; complete strip or sequence. A page may work alone but fail as a spread, or vice
versa. Presentations must preserve live sequence, projection distance, speaker
and audience use, and template role. Documents must preserve content hierarchy,
navigation and intended reading order across pages. Reusable templates define
content ranges, role constraints, safe variation, exception handling and owner
control; a polished sample is not a robust template.

Classify space by job before changing it: within a semantic unit, between peers
or groups, section transition, edge/live-area relation, gutter/fold/binding,
caption/source/annotation attachment, sequence pause/page-turn reveal, focal
framing/direction, or deliberate tension/counterstructure. “More whitespace” is
not a repair for excess content, false grouping, weak hierarchy, wrong measure,
arbitrary containers, trapped gaps, fold conflict or a flat sequence. Optical
relationships may override geometric equality when the intent and protected
constraints remain clear.

Medium-specific decisions transform general craft: how hierarchy and type
measure behave across roles and distance; how crops and crossovers survive trim,
gutter, fold or projection; how colour and material appear in context; how a
page, spread, slide or surface controls entry, continuity and exit. Do not
replace actual type, image, colour or composition judgment with a medium recipe.

## Allocate the content before polishing a page

For adjacent programme, pricing and visitor-information groups, bind each to its
own reading band and check the gap to its neighbours. Internal row alignment
does not close a crossing side note. Judge sparse spreads by reading continuity
and image/caption association, not a required fill ratio or added ornament.

Choose the entry by actual use: a live presentation supports a timed spoken
argument; a reading document carries its explanation without a speaker; a
poster must establish its priority at the relevant distance; a reference sheet
supports lookup. The same source need not have the same allocation in each.

An original flatplan for a three-part report might assign opening question and
scope to the first unit, evidence with its qualifications to the second, and
the decision plus next step to the third. Put actual headings, quantities,
captions and image roles into those units. Now inspect the whole strip: a dense
evidence unit may need a quiet transition, but alternating density mechanically
adds nothing. Compare a compact reference version when readers need all facts
together. Preserve each medium's required content across the complete sequence;
do not move an essential qualification into absent speaker notes. This worked
allocation is a starting comparison, not a required page count or template.

## Critique failure signatures and causes

- **Competent but monotonous sequence:** one template, density, type scale or
  image relation repeats without a sequence thesis. Reassign page/spread roles
  and vary only what their jobs require; preserve anchors.
- **Random or disorienting order:** no flat plan, transitions, folios or parent
  ownership. Restore the smallest recurring orientation system.
- **Crowded despite small type:** content allocation or page count is wrong.
  Edit, redistribute, add a unit, or change structure before shrinking type and
  intervals.
- **Empty but filler worsens it:** focal relation, crop, page role or spatial job
  is weak. Strengthen that relation and preserve useful quiet.
- **Peers split or unrelated items merge:** semantic interval or shared edge is
  wrong. Repair the parent group before boxes, rules or nudges.
- **Every role competes:** consequence is unranked and too many signals are loud.
  Re-rank and quiet support roles rather than add an accent.
- **Spread/fold/panel fails:** gutter, crossover, inside/outside margin, panel
  order, front/back orientation or handling of the publication/display was
  ignored. Repair architecture and protected zones before treatment. Product-
  package assembly and SKU relations belong to Packaging.
- **Type or image works on authoring canvas only:** final scale, distance,
  lighting, substitution, projection or substrate was untested. Refit the
  relationship in the final context; do not invoke universal sizes.
- **Template cannot hold real variation:** ideal sample content defined the
  system. Test shortest, longest, missing, image-led, text-led and exception
  cases, then repair the shared role or constraint.
- **Deterministic checks pass but design is weak:** artifact validity was
  mistaken for hierarchy, grouping, rhythm or meaning. Return to visual cause.

## Smallest repair and regression

Trace `observation → fixed-context effect → page/sequence/geometry cause →
smallest shared owner → preserved strengths → recheck`. Repair content
allocation, unit role, parent/master, group, measure, grid, crop or medium
geometry before local decoration. Preserve exact content, valid page exceptions,
intentional asymmetry, working anchors and source ownership. Recheck identical
content in single units, facing/fold/surface views and the full strip; include
final-size or distance context and reading-order intent. A local repair may not
quietly break another page, slide, fold, template variant or protected region.

## Exceptions

Off-grid placement, overlap, asymmetry, unusual crop, deliberate page break,
variable density, no folio, or a one-off page role is valid when intent, stable
counterstructure, required content/access floor, affected contexts,
compensation and falsifier are recorded. A fixed canvas may optimize for one
priority viewing context if limitations are visible. Supplier conventions may
be departed from only within confirmed technical allowance; the visual
exception does not certify production.

## Proof ladder

1. **Source-valid:** content, order, assets and authority recorded.
2. **Artifact-present:** intended pages/slides/panels/templates and dimensions
   exist; this is not design proof.
3. **Rendered:** all relevant units, spreads, folds, surfaces and variants
   render.
4. **Visually inspected:** hierarchy, type, grouping, spacing, negative space,
   crops, sequence and exceptions judged at the four scopes and final context.
5. **Production/provider/physical:** separate evidence owned by production,
   receiver, venue or specialist; never infer it from the earlier stages.

For handoff, record final size, template/sequence, intended reading order,
protected geometry, fold/surface/display/viewing context, assumptions and exact
proof requested. A valid PDF is not a good layout; a good screen render is not
print, projection, fold or venue approval.

## Ownership and claim ceiling

This leaf owns fixed-medium concept, page/spread/slide/document/template system,
fold/surface and viewing intent for publications and non-navigational displays.
Packaging owns product-package panels, assembled views and SKU graphic systems;
Wayfinding owns physical journeys, destination/decision-point systems and sign
families. General craft owners retain typography, composition, imagery and
colour decisions. Production, provider and relevant specialists own export,
embedding, profiles, preflight, imposition, fabrication, safety and physical
acceptance. Claim only the fixed relationships and render conditions inspected;
do not claim print-ready, provider-approved, accessible, legible at distance,
production-safe or audience-tested without exact evidence.
