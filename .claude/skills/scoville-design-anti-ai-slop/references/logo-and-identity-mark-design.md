# Logo and identity-mark design

Status: `draft`  
Intervention: `focus`

## Load when

Load when creating, comparing, critiquing, or repairing a wordmark, symbol,
monogram, emblem, combination mark, responsive mark family, lockup form, or
optical identity variant. Load when a mark's formal construction or survival
across required contexts can change the result. Do not load for placement,
scaling, export, or strict use of an already approved canonical asset when no
mark form is open. Do not load for functional interface icons, chart symbols,
wayfinding pictograms, naming, brand strategy, or trademark research alone.

## Mark record and authority

For input and configuration records, select fields material to the current
open mark decision or its proof. Keep applicable name/content, incumbent
authority, rights and approval constraints; unrelated fields do not block a
bounded repair.

Require the approved name and spelling, identity thesis, entity and audience,
mark job, brand architecture role, required configurations, incumbent relation,
mandatory retained equity, languages and scripts, touchpoints, smallest-detail
and reproduction contexts, partners, rights state, approval owner, and unknowns.
Mark every input as approved, observed, inferred, proposed, or unknown. Preserve
canonical assets when the incumbent relation is `conform`. An aesthetically
stronger redraw is still unauthorised when the current system does not permit
it.

Record each proposed configuration as:

`identity job | approved name/content | formal mechanism | silhouette |
figure-ground relation | construction logic | custom letter relation |
responsive or optical role | invariant | permitted variation | forbidden
mutation | target contexts | canonical source | evidence | owner/status`.

Brand authority supplies the mark's strategic role, required configuration
set, inheritance, partner hierarchy, approval, migration, and governance. This
leaf does not invent those decisions. A missing manual or source asset is not
permission to redesign.

## Generate and decide

1. Translate the approved identity thesis into one identification job. State
   what must remain recognisable and what contextual pressure the mark must
   survive. Do not start from an unexplained symbol category or style adjective.
2. When formal direction is open, explore materially different mechanisms before
   polishing. For a bounded repair, preserve the accepted mechanism. Difference
   may arise from word form, initial structure, container, figure-ground event,
   spatial relation, rhythm, transformation, object relation, or another
   subject-specific operation. A changed typeface, colour, badge shape, or
   arrangement alone is not a different mechanism. For every candidate, name
   the causal operator and the visible figure-ground or component relation that
   actually differentiates it.
3. Use verified subject and language evidence. Abstract a referent until the
   mark gains identification value, not until it becomes a generic spark,
   globe, shield, leaf, monogram, or geometric loop. Familiar forms remain
   valid when the task benefits from convention and the complete relation is
   specific.
4. Inspect the core form before nonessential treatment can hide it. When the
   required system includes one-colour or reversed use, develop that structure
   before relying on palette, gradient, texture, image, or motion. Judge
   positive and negative mass, figure and ground, outer silhouette, counters,
   openings, joins, tangencies, stroke or shape weight, direction, balance,
   rhythm, and apparent centre. Mathematical construction does not replace
   optical correction.
5. For wordmarks and letter-based marks, draw and space the name as one form.
   Inspect distinctive letters, repeated shapes, joins, counters, terminal
   rhythm, case, abbreviation behaviour, and the relation between custom and
   unmodified glyphs. Preserve reading when identification depends on the name.
   Do not mistake an unexamined typeface distortion for resolved custom craft.
6. Build only the configurations the approved system requires. Examples can
   include primary, horizontal, stacked, symbol-only, wordmark-only, compact,
   monochrome, reversed, or language variants. Every variant has a named
   pressure and a shared invariant. A responsive family is not a collection of
   unrelated redraws.
7. Create optical variants only when scale, process, substrate, or viewing
   condition changes a perceivable relation. Adjust detail, aperture, spacing,
   weight, join, or proportion deliberately while preserving identity. Never
   pretend a simple geometric downscale proves small-size fitness.
8. Compare surviving mechanisms with identical name, colour condition,
   presentation scale, context, and effort. Select through required gates,
   visible tradeoffs, and the highest-risk context. Do not choose from a
   beauty-page presentation that hides weak reduction or lockup behaviour.
9. Reconcile the report with the actual source and render. Verify connected and
   disconnected components, containment, occlusion, counters, negative cuts and
   figure-ground events before describing them. A claimed connection, receiver
   or interlock that is not visibly present is a failed mechanism, not a prose
   explanation of intended geometry.

## Correct a contour without changing its idea

Isolate the suspect contour and decide whether the join is meant to be smooth
or a corner. In a cubic curve, inspect the anchor and its adjacent handles:
their directions govern departure and arrival, while their lengths affect the
curve's pull. Compare one handle change before adding anchors; an unwanted
local bend may need fewer points, while a deliberate cusp must remain. A smooth
join does not require equal handle lengths.

Restore the fill after the outline study. Check whether the corrected contour
also improves the adjoining negative space, apparent weight and silhouette.
Compare positive and reversed versions at the required small size; the same
coordinates can have different apparent weight against different surrounds.
Make a named optical variant only for an observed failure, preserving the
identifying relationship. For a letter-based mark, recheck the neighbouring
letters after changing a terminal or counter. Curve mechanics are technical;
these optical comparisons are local judgment, not proof that fewer points or
geometric smoothness make a better mark.

## Critique failure signatures and causes

- **Generic despite polish.** A gradient orb, swoosh, abstract letter in a
  rounded square, infinite loop, leaf, spark or shield may follow category
  shorthand without a verified subject relation. Identify that interchangeable
  mechanism before adding detail. These motifs are not banned; retain a valid
  incumbent or a familiar form with a real identification job.
- **Idea exists only in explanation.** The verbal rationale supplies meaning
  that the visible form does not carry. Repair the perceivable relation or
  narrow the claim.
- **Silhouette collapses.** Internal detail, narrow gaps, weak figure-ground,
  similar outer contours, or dependent colour carries recognition. Repair mass
  and counterstructure before decoration.
- **One-colour or reverse failure.** Overlap, transparency, outline, gradient,
  or tonal contrast performs structural work. Rebuild the underlying shape
  relation rather than making a cosmetic monochrome export.
- **Small-size fill-in or dropout.** Counters close, joins merge, fine parts
  disappear, spacing collapses, or the name becomes unreadable. Create a
  justified optical variant or reject the mechanism for that role.
- **Wordmark feels typed rather than designed.** Default spacing, unresolved
  repeated forms, awkward joins, accidental terminal relations, or generic
  font substitution carries the result. Repair the word as a connected form.
- **Symbol and wordmark compete.** Both demand recognition, their weight or
  rhythm conflicts, or the gap acts as accidental emptiness. Decide the lead
  and repair their formal relationship.
- **Variants drift.** Each format solves pressure independently and loses the
  shared invariant. Restore the minimum identity relation across the family.
- **Construction diagram performs false authority.** Grids, circles, or ratios
  explain a geometry but not identification, optical balance, or use. Remove
  decorative construction claims and judge the rendered form.
- **Mockups hide defects.** Texture, embossing, photography, perspective, or
  scale makes the mark appear stronger than its source form. Return to neutral
  same-condition comparisons.
- **Similarity is treated as clearance.** A small inspected field, search, or
  model recollection cannot establish distinctiveness or legal safety. Keep
  the result provisional and hand the rights question to its authority.

For every finding record the configuration and context, visible observation,
likely identity effect, confirmed or inferred cause, canonical owner, smallest
repair, preserved strengths, affected variants, evidence needed, and claim
ceiling. Separate a formal defect from a strategic disagreement, deliberate
exception, attributed preference, and unresolved rights risk.

## Repair and regression

Repair the earliest formal cause. Change concept mechanism before detail,
silhouette before interior polish, counterstructure before outline, letter
relations before effects, and the family invariant before local variants.
Alter the canonical source and regenerate affected configurations. Do not patch
exports independently or redraw an incumbent mark from a screenshot.

Preserve approved name, role, equity, architecture, strong silhouette,
distinctive letter or shape relations, valid asymmetry, and authorised optical
exceptions. Recheck neutral source views and only the contexts that actually
apply. Typical pressures include one-colour, reversed, small, large, low
resolution, poor contrast, dense partnership, embroidery, engraving, stamp,
sign, favicon, app tile, motion entry, and long or localised names. A named
context is a test condition, not a mandatory deliverable.

Compare before and after at identical scale and rendering conditions. Recheck
every dependent lockup when proportion, spacing, baseline, mark weight, or
canonical geometry changes.

## Rules and deliberate exceptions

Binding constraints include approved identity role, exact name, incumbent
authority, canonical assets, required configurations, partner agreements,
protected cultural meaning, permissions, and release state. Figure-ground,
counter integrity, optical balance, configuration coherence, and contextual
survival are decision criteria, not prescriptions for minimalism.

There is no universal golden ratio, construction grid, clear-space multiple,
minimum pixel size, concept count, colour count, simplicity level, symmetry
rule, or requirement that a symbol work without its name. A detailed emblem,
irregular wordmark, variable mark, intentional ambiguity, unusual spacing, or
context-specific configuration can be valid when its identification job,
protected content, target contexts, compensation, owner, and falsifier are
recorded. An exception cannot waive rights, cultural authority, required
partner treatment, or an incumbent CI rule.

## Proof, ownership, and claim ceiling

For each supplied lockup, separately name its internal symbol/word relation
and its relation to the surrounding field. Bind the Core's object, region or
anchor, intent/target, Mark owner, metric/view and evidence status to that
configuration. Word-to-panel containment cannot prove symbol-to-word clearance;
the main lockup's gap cannot stand in for a small or monochrome variant.
Inspect each relevant local relation and measure it in the actual configuration.
For browser-rendered SVG, the optional
[`inventory-svg-layout.js`](../scripts/inventory-svg-layout.js) can expose
adjacent artwork/text candidates before these choices. Its queue is geometric
discovery, not a verdict on ink, optical spacing, intent or identity preservation.

Evidence can include the editable canonical source, construction and optical
notes, same-condition mechanism comparison, one-colour and reversed sources,
smallest-detail render, relevant process simulation or sample, complete
configuration sheet, partner and language states, before-and-after comparison,
and approval status. Inspect vectors at outline level and renders at intended
size. A large presentation board does not prove small use, and a simulated
emboss does not prove manufacture.

This leaf owns formal identity-mark mechanisms, configuration construction,
wordmark craft, symbol-wordmark relationships, responsive families, and
optical variants. Brand owns role, architecture, required configurations,
governance, migration, and approval. Typography owns the general type system.
Culture and affected communities own sensitive meaning. Asset Rights and
qualified counsel own permission, similarity, trademark, and legal conclusions.
Production and the receiver own delivered-file and process acceptance.

Claim only what the inspected field and rendered contexts show, such as
`formally distinct within compared proposals`, `one-colour inspected`,
`small-size render inspected`, or `configuration family inspected`. Do not
claim originality, trademark availability, non-infringement, universal
recognition, memorability, cultural approval, accessibility, fabrication
fitness, or market distinctiveness without the responsible evidence and owner.
