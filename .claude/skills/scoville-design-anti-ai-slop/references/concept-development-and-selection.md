# Concept development and selection

Status: `draft`  
Intervention: `focus`  
Sources: `SRC-CONCEPT-EVIDENCE`, `SRC-BRIEF-HCD`, `SRC-FOUNDATION-GRAPHIC`, `SRC-PACKAGE-LOCAL-SYNTHESIS`

## Load when

Load when materially different ideas, concept territories, organising
mechanisms, evidence-bearing roughs, selection, or concept-level repair can
change the outcome. Do not load to execute an accepted direction, make surface
variants, choose a named style, or polish domain craft.

## Concept record and inputs

Require the available outcome, audience/context, communication barrier,
required content, incumbent system, hard gates, comparative criteria,
deliverable set, access/production constraints, and unknowns. Preserve exact
content and owner constraints; never complete missing research or strategy from
model familiarity.

A concept is a repeatable relationship among **subject evidence**, **required
message**, **audience action or interpretation**, and one or more **carriers**.
A palette or typeface alone does not establish a new conceptual mechanism;
its visual treatment may still be the right design solution. During exploration
use `idea | subject connection | intended effect`. Expand only decision-changing
questions at selection: content order, carrier, counter-reading, invariant,
medium advantage, incumbent relation or highest-risk unknown. These are prompts,
not a form to complete before trying a rough.

Carriers may include rhetoric or language, sequence, comparison, reveal, index,
route, transformation, typographic behaviour, image/object/material relation,
spatial relation, data encoding, interaction, or time. Name how each delivers
the thesis; do not make every visible decision “the concept.”

For an open differentiation task, bind a verified supplied or observed subject
fact to each territory's primary carrier. If specificity judgment is also open,
select Generic through Core and use its ladder; do not load a sibling here or
duplicate that rubric. A labelled inference may support a hypothesis, not an
approved fact. Quiet utility and accepted directions need no artificial score.

Allocate text and image complementary jobs unless intentional repetition has a
named identification, access, memory or error-prevention function. When keeping
a cue is open, inspect its intended distinguishing role at output size before
treating it as noise; retain, simplify or omit by that job. Recognition benefits
remain hypotheses without audience evidence. Adjacency implies a relation;
state the intended one and the consequential alternative reading. Ambiguity can
carry a concept only when the intended audience can plausibly resolve it and
protected facts, identity, action, warning and disclosure remain recoverable.

## Generate materially different territories

When different mechanisms are needed, vary content order, audience action,
meaning construction, primary carrier, format behaviour or central risk.
Temporarily hiding colour, font and texture can test mechanism diversity; restore
them to judge the actual design's communication and character. A treatment can
win that judgment without being a new mechanism. Explore only alternatives that
could change the choice; neither mechanism count nor novelty proves quality.

Abstract references into bounded jobs and rejected or copy-risk traits. Do not
clone one precedent, average several into a trend centroid, or call search
absence originality. When familiarity, safety, speed, repeated use, or task
clarity matters, include a quiet utility direction whose concept may be error
prevention, direct comparison, progressive disclosure, stable convention, or
one decision at a time.

Instantiate each surviving territory at the cheapest fidelity that exposes its
carrier: content map, thumbnail, storyboard, wireflow, material mock, motion
beat, or rough. Use the same real content, comparable effort, fidelity and
conditions; show candidates side by side before refinement. A polished comp may
not compete against two descriptions.

## Make the difference visible

When the opening of a page or presentation is unresolved, compare viable entry
forms using the same supplied content: a statement, image, demonstration,
comparison or interaction. Which makes the subject and the audience's first
question most immediately recoverable? Rough out only alternatives that could
change the choice, at comparable fidelity. This is a probe within concept
selection, not a required five-option exercise or permission to add unsupported
content, assets or functionality.

For a fictional, approved proposition “Borrow the object, keep the space”, one
mechanism could show a tool-sized opening in a crowded cupboard; another could
show the same tool moving between several users. Absence carries the first
idea, transfer carries the second. Render enough of each relation to test that
distinction. Changing the opening's colour or typeface is a near-variant of the
first mechanism, not a third idea. A literal object-and-availability list can
still be the best direction for a utility task. This original example teaches
mechanism discrimination; it does not establish the proposition as a fact,
require two concepts or authorise new audience claims.

For a compact control, compare the [decoration-control source](../examples/spatial-proof/decoration-control.svg)
with its [render](../examples/spatial-proof/decoration-control.png). It keeps the
subject-bearing route and removes an unrelated device; this is a purpose test,
not a general instruction to make designs sparse.

## Select without false precision

Apply non-negotiable gates first. For survivors, compare each criterion with
`observation | evidence | uncertainty | trade-off | owner`; do not let a weighted
total end the judgment. Test the uncertainty most likely to reverse selection.
Allowed outcomes are `select`, `develop`, `test`, `combine`, `defer`, `reject`,
or `reframe`. Combine only when the carrier logic remains coherent rather than
forming a compromise collage. Record the decision owner, dissent, rejected
reasons, preserved strengths, next proof and claim ceiling.

Stress the highest-risk representative conditions: smallest or densest, largest
or distance view, short/long/localised content, static/interactive, motion and
reduced equivalent, monochrome, print/screen, or system state as relevant. The
invariant and carrier must survive; pixel-identical composition need not.

## Critique, cause and smallest repair

- **Font/palette siblings:** one mechanism received several costumes. Change
  the weaker content order, audience action, carrier, or meaning construction;
  preserve valid subject evidence.
- **Generic thesis:** it fits an unrelated subject after a logo swap. Bind one
  verified subject process, object, language, data, tension, or behaviour to the
  carrier; reject the territory if only naming makes it specific.
- **Explanation outranks the image:** compare visible type, colour, composition
  and image craft with idea fit. Neither a literal subject link nor one superior
  component decides the whole. State the brief-based trade-off; preserve
  convincing expression and quiet utility, including strengths of rejected work.
- **Polish wins:** effort or presentation order anchored judgment. Normalise
  fidelity or develop all survivors to the same decision-relevant evidence.
- **Required content disappears:** the mechanism assumes ideal brevity. Restore
  exact content and repair allocation or carrier; reject if the concept cannot
  carry it.
- **Utility becomes theatrical:** distinctiveness was treated as a gate. Return
  to task clarity, familiarity, recovery or system continuity; remove friction.
- **Format collapse:** meaning depends on one canvas or sensory channel. Move it
  to an adaptable carrier and define context-native expressions.
- **Matrix hides disagreement:** unknowns or preferences were scored as facts.
  Restore gates, uncertainty, trade-offs and named authority.

Repair the smallest parent cause - frame, subject relation, carrier, rough,
selection judgment, or format mapping - while preserving the selected thesis,
verified content, working relationships and incumbent constraints. Re-render
only affected conditions, then regression-check the complete representative
set. If a rough exposes a false frame, record the change and regenerate affected
alternatives; never silently revise criteria.

## Exceptions

One direction may be sufficient for a mature system, tiny reversible change,
production emergency, narrow commissioned expression, or evidence-backed path.
Familiar patterns and a low-profile concept are valid when novelty would harm
use. A concept may favour one priority format if the limitation and alternate
conditions are explicit. Deliberate style dependence is valid when style is a
named carrier with a causal job, not a substitute for the communication thesis.

## Proof, ownership and claim ceiling

Evidence is a frozen input/content set, pairwise mechanism comparison,
same-content rough set, gate/trade-off record, highest-risk format/access/
production check, decision and rejection rationale, repair history, and
remaining unknowns. Apply the Core's separate proof lanes.

This leaf owns concept mechanisms, comparable instantiation, selection and
concept repair. The decision frame owns criteria; craft owners execute type,
composition, image, colour, information, interaction and motion; providers and
qualified authorities prove their domains. Claim only subject specificity,
difference within the inspected set, or bounded prior-art findings actually
shown. Never claim globally original, memorable, audience-validated,
trademark-safe, non-infringing, feasible, accessible, or production-ready from
concept comparison alone.
