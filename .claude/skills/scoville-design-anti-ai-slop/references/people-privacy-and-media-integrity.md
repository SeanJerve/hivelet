# People, privacy, and media integrity

Status: `draft`  
Intervention: `external-verification`  
Sources: `SRC-PEOPLE-PRIVACY`, `SRC-IMAGE-INTEGRITY`, `SRC-IMAGE-METADATA-ACCESS`, `SRC-SYNTHETIC-PROVENANCE`, `SRC-ASSET-RIGHTS`, `SRC-PACKAGE-LOCAL-SYNTHESIS`

## Load when

Load when identifiable people, minors, bystanders, private places or data,
release scope, re-identification, embedded metadata, documentary/editorial
integrity, constructed scenes, generated/reconstructed media, or factual-mode
disclosure can change the work. It also applies to synthetic evidence without a
depicted person. Do not load for clearly fictional decoration that cannot
reasonably be read as evidence or identity and carries no participant/privacy
condition.

## Inputs and decision record

Declare the media mode: documentary/editorial evidence, constructed or staged,
illustrative, composite, synthetic, reconstruction, or unknown. Record source
and edit history; depicted and affected people; minors, bystanders and
vulnerable participants; capture/generation context; intended audience,
territory, duration, placement and downstream reuse; consent/release authority
and limits; privacy expectations; sensitive attributes and locations;
caption/disclosure; metadata; model/provider conditions; factual source; and
approvers. Keep truth status, consent, privacy, publicity, copyright, trademark,
cultural authority and production proof as separate fields.

For each person or sensitive condition record what was agreed, by whom, when,
for which media/use/territory/duration, whether withdrawal or review exists,
what transformations are permitted, and which uncertainty blocks release.
Consent to capture is not automatically consent to publication, generation,
training, compositing, advertising, political implication or indefinite reuse.

## Generate or decide

1. Select the mode before treatment. Write permitted and prohibited operations
   for crop, tone/colour, cleanup, retouching, object/person removal/addition,
   compositing, reconstruction, voice/text generation and captioning.
2. Protect participant agency. Minimize collection and exposure, avoid
   coercive or incompatible reuse, give meaningful review or withdrawal where
   the governing process requires it, and make unresolved power or safety risk
   visible to the accountable owner.
3. For documentary/editorial work, preserve event, identity, place, time,
   scale, relationship and material context needed to support the factual job.
   Do not add, remove or synthesize evidence unless the governing policy
   expressly permits it and the result cannot mislead in context.
4. For constructed, composite or synthetic work, prevent fabricated people,
   products, quotations, documents, locations, outcomes or endorsements from
   being read as factual evidence. Use a disclosure whose wording, placement,
   persistence and accessibility match the risk and destination.
5. Use the least revealing source and treatment that still accomplishes the
   legitimate job. Consider substitution, aggregation, wider crop, silhouette,
   illustration, redaction, delayed release or withholding before relying on
   blur alone.
6. Define metadata policy for source and every derivative: EXIF/GPS, device and
   time, names, document properties, comments, layers, revision history,
   thumbnails, embedded source assets, accessibility metadata and provenance
   manifests. Remove only what policy permits; retain required authorship,
   rights, disclosure and accessibility information.
7. Carry the mode, permitted-operation and disclosure record through crops,
   translations, thumbnails, social previews, exports and handoff. A derivative
   must not lose the condition that made the source acceptable.

## Critique: failure signatures and causes

- **Participant is technically blurred but still identifiable:** clothing,
  body, voice, companions, location, schedule, metadata or surrounding text
  enables inference; anonymity was treated as one pixel effect.
- **A minor or bystander appears in a promotional context:** no specific
  authority, purpose limitation, safety review or downstream-use boundary.
- **A plausible reconstruction reads as capture:** realism, caption, layout or
  placement implies evidence while mode/disclosure is absent or separated.
- **Documentary source has altered meaning:** removal, generative fill, crop,
  sequencing, retouching, colour treatment or caption changed material context.
- **Disclosure exists but fails:** it is vague, inaccessible, transient,
  cropped from derivatives, or does not name the relevant synthetic/constructed
  condition.
- **Release is present but use is unsupported:** person/use/territory/duration,
  media, modification, sublicensing, endorsement or withdrawal scope does not
  match the delivery.
- **Metadata exposes a person or private place:** GPS, timestamp, document
  author, revision, layer, thumbnail or linked-source data survived export.
- **A “safe” stock or generated person encodes a real identity or stereotype:**
  source, likeness, representational and rights questions were collapsed into
  provider compliance.

Record exact artifact/location, observation, likely harm or factual effect,
affected people, authority, cause as confirmed/inferred/unknown, severity and
confidence, smallest repair, preserved evidence/context, and unresolved release
state. Do not diagnose legal compliance from appearance.

## Repair and regression

Repair the failing condition rather than cosmetically masking it: remove or
replace the asset; narrow audience/use/territory/duration; obtain accountable
authority; change crop or context; redact all re-identifying channels; restore
documentary source; label the actual construction; or withhold release. If a
source or policy changes, rebuild every dependent derivative and invalidate
stale receipts.

Preserve participant dignity and agency, authorised purpose, material factual
context, evidentiary pixels, required rights/credit and accessible meaning.
Inspect every output size and channel, adjacent headline/caption, search/social
preview, alt text, translated version, metadata package and handoff. Verify
that a repair does not create a false implication by omission.

## Rules and exceptions

Binding constraints are the declared mode, supplied facts and sources,
participant conditions, governing editorial or organisational policy, approved
release scope, current provider/contract duties, privacy/accessibility
requirements and no invented clearance. Current laws and platform terms are
jurisdiction- and use-specific and must be checked by their accountable owner.

Documentary conventions may permit bounded tonal, crop or technical changes;
commercial illustration may permit extensive construction. Neither convention
transfers automatically. A reconstruction, anonymized case, public-interest
publication, archival image or synthetic representative may be justified only
when mandate, necessity, proportionality, affected-person risk, alternatives,
disclosure, source integrity, authority and expiry are recorded. The exception
does not prove consent, truth, legality or safety.

## Proof, ownership and claim ceiling

Compare original and final pixels, crop, sequence, caption and context. Inspect
metadata from the delivered file rather than only the working document. Resolve
participant/release records to the exact use; check disclosure in every
derivative; record source, edits, tools and evidence state. Signed provenance
can support association and edit history but not truth, identity, permission or
quality. Blur, pixelation, a stock license, a model safety setting or a label
alone cannot prove anonymity or clearance.

This module owns mode policy, participant conditions, privacy, permitted
transformations, documentary/synthetic integrity, disclosure and metadata
safety. Imagery owns visual selection/treatment. Sources owns factual support.
Asset Rights owns use-specific permission/credit records. Culture owns
representational authority. Counsel, editors, safeguarding leads, affected
people and other accountable authorities decide matters beyond design evidence.
Report only the inspected conditions; never claim consent, anonymity, legal
clearance, factual truth or universal safety from the artifact alone.
