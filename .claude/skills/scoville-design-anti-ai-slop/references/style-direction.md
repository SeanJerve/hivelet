# Style direction

Status: `draft`  
Intervention: `focus`  
Sources: `SRC-STYLE-HISTORY`, `SRC-FOUNDATION-GRAPHIC`, `SRC-CULTURE-AUTHORITY`, `SRC-ASSET-RIGHTS`, `SRC-PACKAGE-LOCAL-SYNTHESIS`

## Load when

Load when a period, movement, genre, regional or vernacular language,
subculture, material/process aesthetic, named visual direction, revival, or
cross-medium style translation can change the work; also load for an open
unnamed style grammar whose tool/trend defaults need a structural decision.
Do not load for generic
adjectives such as clean or premium, an ordinary incumbent-system execution,
base typography/composition craft alone, or a named style used only as casual
inspiration with no consequential design decision.

## Disambiguate before designing

A style label is not a preset. Select only open relevant Style Record fields;
otherwise use the Core minimal record:

`requested term/language | class | aliases | selected sense | plausible rejected
senses | date/place/community/status | source/evidence state | confidence and
contradiction | dominant lineage or incumbent system | bounded influences |
purpose/context | production/material cause | structural DNA | subject relation |
recognition/cliché hypothesis | revival/anachronism state | brand invariants |
rights/cultural state | medium mappings | proof and authority`.

Class may be `movement/lineage`, `period umbrella`, `genre/aesthetic`,
`method/reduction`, `material/process`, `mood/attribute`, `regional/vernacular`,
`subculture`, or `incumbent system`. Mark claims `supplied`, `verified`,
`inferred`, `contested`, `unknown`, or `community authority required`. When a
label has materially different senses - such as 1980s, Brutalist, Minimalist or
Neoclassical - present the bounded alternatives that fit the brief and choose
one; do not blend the currently fashionable associations invisibly.

Choose one dominant lineage or incumbent system. Give each secondary influence
or modifier one structural, content or medium job; remove equal-weight moodboard
ingredients. Declare the treatment as `period-grounded interpretation`,
`documented revival`, `contemporary translation`, `deliberate anachronism`, or
`hybrid`. Visual resemblance alone does not prove date, geography, production,
authenticity or community recognition.

## Compile style DNA into a usable system

Connect evidenced purpose, context and production cause to the **smallest set
of load-bearing relations** that differentiates the direction and serves this
subject:

- hierarchy, grouping, grid or anti-grid, density, rhythm, negative-space job,
  edge behaviour, scale, overlap/crop, symmetry/asymmetry, sequence and order;
- semantic type roles, voice, proportion, density, casing/punctuation, script
  and language behaviour, plus period/process relation;
- colour roles, value/contrast structure, material/light, reproduction cause,
  channel limit and reduced or monochrome equivalent;
- image, mark, symbol, ornament, texture and material roles, including what they
  depict, who may interpret/use them and what process causes their appearance;
- motion, sound and interaction behaviour only when the direction and medium
  actually require them.

Do not fill every category with a trope. A print defect, halftone,
misregistration, monospacing, noise or rough edge needs an original causal job
and a valid target-medium analogue; otherwise it is costume. Detailed
typography, spacing, colour, imagery, interaction and production remain their
owners' decisions. A named label never implies a font family, pairing, palette,
spacing scale, grid, motif count, breakpoint or animation recipe.

State how subject evidence - process, object, language, data, ritual, tension or
behaviour - changes the system. Add a counterweight only to repair an observed
functional conflict. Add a signature only when verified subject evidence can
carry it; zero or several coordinated expressions may be right.

For every reference item used beyond private inspiration, record creator,
title, date, institution/owner, stable identifier, exact asset status or
license, intended transformation, attribution, and unresolved trademark,
privacy/publicity or cultural conditions. A collection page, open-access label,
repository license or museum taxonomy is not item permission. Living,
community-held, sacred, secret or contested material requires the actual
authority; stop, narrow, consult or replace when it is absent.

## Generate and translate

Generate the whole artifact from the Style Record and real content, not by
swapping palette, font, texture or symbols onto a generic composition. Preserve
the communication problem, required content, access floor and incumbent brand
or product invariants; choose preserve, extend, reinterpret, bounded campaign
exception, or owner-approved change.

For each destination medium map:
`invariant relation → medium-native carrier → allowed variation → lost
affordance → equivalent/compensation → access/production proof`. Recompose
relationships rather than copy pixels. A print misregistration idea might
become a time/state separation only if the production logic and meaning survive;
do not animate a defect merely to look retro. Poster, mobile, motion, print,
sound, reduced mode and fixed display may express different components while
retaining the same causal grammar.

## Translate a sourced relation, not a surface effect

Fictional supplied process brief: two flat ink passes and shapes cut from paper.
A usable relation is the overlap of two bounded silhouettes, with the second
pass revealing a meaningful shared region. A poster may use that region to
carry its key word; a compact screen notice may preserve the same overlap and
word relation while changing scale and allocation. Do not add random distress
to simulate age, or claim that the construction authenticates a historical
movement. This original process example is bounded by its supplied premise.
For a real named lineage, inspect an actual source before adopting its relation;
retain the relevant cultural and item-rights authority. If a plain incumbent
system already serves the task, no style translation is required.

## Critique failure signatures and causes

- **Style equals obvious motif bundle:** a label was mapped to stereotype.
  Remove the loudest cue and strengthen one sourced structural or production
  relation tied to the subject.
- **1980s equals synthwave/VHS; Neoclassical equals black/gold/columns;
  Minimalist equals cream/serif/empty bento; Brutalist equals terminal chrome:**
  a broad or disputed sense collapsed to internet shorthand. Select and source
  the intended branch, then repair hierarchy, type, material and context.
- **Random hybrid:** no dominant lineage and influences have no jobs. Choose a
  dominant system, bound or remove each influence, and preserve coherent parts.
- **Pasted-on distress or imitation:** surface has no production cause. Recreate
  the underlying relation with an appropriate process, label the simulation, or
  remove it.
- **False period accuracy:** later type, technology or convention is undeclared.
  Reclassify as revival/translation/curated anachronism or replace unsupported
  elements.
- **Recognisable only by one famous sign:** structural DNA is weak. Keep the cue
  if it orients, but repair the bundle so the removal test remains coherent.
- **Anti-cliché becomes opposite-fashion generic:** ban lists replaced subject
  reasoning. Restore familiar cues that serve recognition and strengthen one
  subject-specific relation.
- **No named style, but a current default look:** mesh gradients, glass panels,
  rounded everything, sparkles or isometric props may be an unexamined tool or
  trend default. When style grammar is open, identify a dominant lineage or a
  subject-derived structural cause and give it a job. Preserve a legitimate
  incumbent/utility direction; familiarity alone does not justify intervention.
- **Style harms task, access or incumbent identity:** it overrode higher
  constraints. Restore content, recovery, contrast/non-colour meaning, reading
  order, script behaviour and owner invariants; constrain or obtain an exception.
- **One medium works, another collapses:** components were copied instead of
  relations translated. Repair the invariant-to-carrier map and recompose.

## Smallest repair and regression

Trace `observation → communication/recognition effect → factual, structural,
subject, production, brand, access, cultural or rights cause → smallest repair
→ preserved strength → new proof`. Repair by relabeling, removing, replacing,
restructuring, re-sourcing, compensating, obtaining authority, or stopping - not
by adding more motifs. Preserve verified history, useful recognition cues,
subject-specific relations, strong hierarchy and incumbent invariants. Re-render
affected media with identical content, then check the whole format set for
style coherence, task/access regressions and accidental new claims.

## Exceptions and removal test

Cliché, anachronism, hybridization, faithful reproduction, stylistic neutrality,
one-medium optimization, unfamiliar cues or controlled brand departure may be
intentional. Declare purpose and authority, broken convention, protected floor,
compensating relation, affected contexts and falsifier. Hide or remove the most
obvious recognition cue: the direction should retain a coherent structural,
material or production relation, but need not remain equally recognisable.
Conversely, preserve a familiar cue when it genuinely helps the intended
audience. The test diagnoses dependence; it is not a score or ban.

## Proof, ownership and claim ceiling

Inspect real-content artifacts at intended size, distance, device or substrate;
compare relevant narrow/wide/fixed/motion/reduced variants; verify type and
spatial relationships, reference and item records, incumbent invariants, and
the removal test. Keep source, render, audience recognition, cultural authority,
rights, access and provider evidence separate. A convincing render proves none
of the other lanes.

This leaf owns interpretation, source-bounded style DNA, anti-cliché reasoning
and medium translation. Concept owns communication mechanism; Brand owns
durable identity; craft and implementation owners retain their decisions;
historians, affected communities, rights holders and providers retain authority.
Do not claim exhaustive style coverage, authentic, period-accurate,
community-approved, rights-cleared, accessible, responsive, production-ready,
original, non-infringing, universally recognisable or professionally superior
without exact independent evidence. No named-style atlas or profile is runtime
authority here.
