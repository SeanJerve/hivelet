# Asset rights and attribution

Status: `draft`  
Intervention: `external-verification`  
Sources: `SRC-ASSET-RIGHTS`, `SRC-PEOPLE-PRIVACY`, `SRC-CULTURE-AUTHORITY`, `SRC-IMAGE-METADATA-ACCESS`, `SRC-SYNTHETIC-PROVENANCE`, `SRC-PRODUCTION-DIGITAL`, `SRC-PACKAGE-LOCAL-SYNTHESIS`

## Load when

Load when an external or generated image, font, icon, illustration, audio,
video, text excerpt, data distribution, screenshot, template, code/media asset
or embedded ingredient will ship and its origin, permission, modification,
distribution or credit is open. Also load when a required notice must survive
delivery. Do not load for bibliographic inspiration that contributes no shipped
asset, or for an internally authored asset whose relevant authority and
downstream use are already documented. Jurisdiction and provider terms modify
an activated rights decision; neither routes this module alone.

## Inputs, authority and formal variables

Identify each in-scope distributable asset and ingredient unambiguously by
path/item ID and version; add a hash when byte identity, reproducible handoff,
audit or a binding requirement depends on it. A local draft needs only the
record sufficient for its actual use and open rights decisions, not a release
ledger for unrelated assets. Draft status never waives required permission or
notices; missing mandatory authority blocks the affected use or release.
Record applicable asset type and exact scope; creator, foundry, producer, publisher,
rightsholder, licensor and provider; source item/version; acquisition date and
receipt; exact licence name/version, legal text, SPDX expression or contract;
licensor authority and known exceptions; model/provider/version and input
references when generated; modifications and build history; and dependent
artifacts.

Classify the intended use before deciding status: purpose; commercial,
editorial, internal or evaluation context; audience; medium/channel; territory;
duration; scale; paid promotion; distribution, embedding, subsetting,
modification, translation, adaptation, sublicensing, resale and template reuse;
downstream recipients; credit/notice location; modification notice; expiry and
recheck owner.

Track separate layers for copyright, contract/provider terms, font licence,
trademark and endorsement, depicted person/privacy/publicity/release, property,
architecture/art/product, data/database, cultural authority/protocol, moral
rights, and confidentiality. One layer never clears another. Empty material
fields are `unknown`, not silently `not applicable`.

## Generate or decide

1. Inventory root files and embedded ingredients. A repository, collection,
   stock-site or template licence does not automatically cover each font,
   screenshot, figure, dataset, image, reference input or third-party element.
2. Resolve the exact grant or authority to the exact asset and intended use.
   Preserve the canonical instrument/version or contract receipt; a badge,
   footer, `free`, `royalty-free`, `open access`, `public domain` or generic `CC`
   label is insufficient.
3. Apply conditions rather than mnemonics. For Creative Commons preserve
   supplied creator, title/source, licence link, notices and change indication,
   and assess NC, ND, SA and compatibility against the actual material, sharing,
   adaptation and purpose. Do not infer clearance of embedded or depicted
   rights.
4. For fonts distinguish ordinary use/embedding from bundling, subsetting,
   modifying and redistributing the font files. Record file/version and hash
   where needed for identity,
   embedding flags or EULA, copyright/licence notices, Reserved Font Names,
   modification/build state and fallback redistribution. The font licence does
   not license the surrounding design.
5. For generated assets record provider/model/version/date, terms receipt,
   inputs/references, relevant consent/authority, human selection/edit history,
   output identifier/hash as needed, disclosure and provenance state. Provider access or a valid
   content credential is not ownership, authorship, truth or non-infringement.
6. Compile required credit and notices into the actual visible, metadata,
   about-screen, end-credit or package location. Make them legible, accessible
   and durable through crops, exports, translations and recipient workflows.
7. Assign `cleared for stated use`, `cleared with conditions`, `restricted`,
   `rejected` or `unverified/escalate`. Fail closed on any mandatory unknown:
   narrow/replace the asset or withhold the affected release.

## Critique: failure signatures and causes

- **Root-licence assumption:** embedded image, font, dataset, screenshot or
  fixture has no asset receipt; package-level terms were applied to all files.
- **Use mismatch:** an editorial/internal/evaluation grant is reused for an ad,
  merchandise, redistribution, template or new territory/term.
- **Layer collapse:** licensed photograph contains a person, artwork, product,
  logo, building or restricted cultural material; capture copyright was treated
  as complete clearance.
- **Licence slogan:** BY/NC/ND/SA, CC0, OFL or “royalty-free” is reduced to a
  universal rule; exact version, material, adaptation or contract was not read.
- **Font permission mismatch:** visual use, web/document embedding, bundling,
  modification and redistribution are conflated; required notice, build history
  or Reserved Font Name condition is lost.
- **Generated equals free/owned:** output status is inferred from the tool,
  provider promise or provenance credential; reference, likeness, trademark,
  contract and jurisdiction layers remain open.
- **Credit only in the ledger:** shipped crop, PDF, site, metadata or package
  omits/truncates creator, source, licence, notice or change indication.
- **Stale or broken receipt:** asset, legal text, rightsholder, provider term or
  use changed; no version, expiry, dependency or recheck existed.

Localise the exact asset, ingredient, use and output. State observation, likely
permission/credit effect, authority, cause as confirmed/inferred/unknown,
severity and confidence. Do not issue a legal conclusion from file metadata or
appearance.

## Smallest repair and regression

Repair the first failing layer: replace or remove the asset; narrow purpose,
channel, territory, duration, modification or downstream use; obtain documented
authority; restore the original; rebuild a compliant font; correct the receipt;
or compile the missing credit/notice. Rebuild every affected derivative from an
authorised source and invalidate stale receipts rather than patching one export.

Preserve intended communication, verified provenance, creator identity,
rightsholder conditions, participant/cultural restrictions, source assets and
prior decision history. Reinventory ingredients and inspect every delivered
crop, format, translation, metadata record, font package, credits view, archive
and handoff. Confirm that replacement did not introduce a new rights layer or
orphan required attribution.

## Rules and exceptions

Binding constraints are the exact asset and version, verified authority,
licence/contract, intended use, participant/cultural conditions, required
notices, current provider terms and applicable current jurisdiction. Public
access, purchase, possession, a checksum, metadata, C2PA, SPDX or automated
coverage can support records but cannot prove ownership, authority, legal
interpretation, missing-asset absence or non-infringement.

An exception or limitation, incidental inclusion, quotation, parody,
documentary/editorial use, voluntary omission of nonrequired credit, or
confidential contract can alter the record only when exact material, use,
jurisdiction, authority, rationale, conditions, evidence, expiry and dependent
outputs are documented. Do not infer or recommend consequential legal
exceptions without qualified review.

## Proof, ownership and claim ceiling

Resolve each shipped asset/ingredient to its receipt and exact use. Inspect the
current instrument or contract, rightsholder authority, file/version identity,
modification history, conditions and all rights layers. Compare generated
credits/notices with delivered HTML, PDF, media, metadata and package; verify
legibility, accessibility and survival after export and recipient processing.
Machine checks prove declared identity, coverage and notice consistency only.

This module owns asset registry, use-specific permission boundary, layered
rights record, attribution and survival requirements. People and cultural
authorities own their conditions; source owners validate facts; production
owners prove output survival; qualified counsel decides consequential legal
interpretation. This is not legal advice. Report only the exact reviewed asset,
use, territory, term and evidence. Never claim ownership, universal clearance,
non-infringement, valid exception or complete rightsholder discovery.
