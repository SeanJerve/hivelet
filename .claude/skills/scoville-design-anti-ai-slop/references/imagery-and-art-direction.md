## Load when

Load when image purpose, photographic or illustrative mode, selection,
sequence, crop, commissioning/capture/generation brief, treatment, or a
multi-image system can change the work. Do not load for an approved incidental
asset whose selection, crop, truth status, treatment and use are all settled.

## Inputs and formal variables

Select the fields below that are material to the current open image decision
or its proof. Keep applicable protected content, source authority, rights and
consent requirements; unrelated fields are not prerequisites for a local edit.

Define the image job: evidence, explanation, identification, atmosphere,
demonstration, persuasion, continuity, texture, or decoration. Record audience,
message, medium, target sizes/aspect ratios, text relationship, viewing
distance, sequence position, source/contact sheet, required and prohibited
content, protected subject/context, truth mode, participant conditions, rights
state, accessibility need, and reproduction destination.

For photographs inspect subject, moment, gesture, viewpoint, lens/perspective,
distance, frame, focal plane, depth, light direction/quality, tonal range,
colour, background, motion, scale reference, noise and usable resolution. For
illustration inspect abstraction, fidelity, silhouette, construction, line,
shape, texture, palette, perspective, scale, edge language, character/object
continuity, symbolic load and reproducibility. For every image record focal
region, crop-safe and no-crop regions, meaningful context, occlusion, text-safe
area and variant limits.

## Generate or decide

1. Write one image thesis: what the viewer must learn or feel, why imagery is
   the right carrier, a plausible competing reading, and what would make it
   misleading or generic. Judge atmosphere or decoration by its declared job;
   neither must explain every instruction.
2. Choose and declare the mode - documentary/editorial, constructed,
   illustrative, diagrammatic, composite, or synthetic - before selecting the
   visual treatment. Do not let realism imply an evidence status the source
   does not possess.
3. Build a contact sheet or comparable field. Reject near-duplicates and rank
   candidates by communication job, subject/context integrity, formal strength,
   text relationship, sequence contribution, variant survival and rights/risk
   state. A beautiful image that fails the job is not the winner.
4. For a commission, capture or generation brief, specify subject and action,
   setting/context, viewpoint, light, palette/treatment, composition, target
   ratios, continuity anchors, protected facts, prohibited inventions,
   references by mechanism rather than imitation, deliverables, review gates
   and proof. Separate must-have content from negotiable style.
5. Direct a system through invariants and controlled variables. A stroke-based
   illustration needs a deliberate weight/terminal grammar; hierarchy or
   expressive variation is valid when purposeful and readable at output size.
   For literal botanical or product identification, inspect appropriate subject
   references; without them, use explicitly abstract treatment and withhold
   identification claims. Keep the
   relevant viewpoint, light logic, abstraction, edge/texture language,
   palette relation, subject treatment or narrative role coherent; vary crop,
   scale, rhythm and emphasis enough to avoid cloned compositions.
6. Compose image and text together using live copy. Protect faces, hands,
   products, evidence, captions and meaningful context; position text from
   actual tonal/texture conditions rather than an assumed “empty” corner.
7. Derive crops from the subject and message. Recompose or select another
   source when a narrow crop destroys the relation; do not keep zooming until
   the subject technically fits.

## Choose operations for the image's job

For a photograph or a proposed capture, distinguish viewpoint, subject distance
and framing. A higher viewpoint may reveal an object's working surface; a
lower one may conceal it. Compare whether front, side or back light reveals
the relevant volume, texture or silhouette. Decide this from the subject and
permitted truth mode, not a mood word. A crop cannot restore missing context;
return to the source or choose a different image when necessary.

For illustration, block masses and silhouette before detail; resolve the
overlap, contact, scale or construction carrying the idea. Inspect a dominant
recognizable object at native detail even when atmospheric: proportions,
major-part alignment and projection must form a coherent treatment. Distinguish
rotation or deliberate stylization from accidental stretching. Compare a
suitable subject reference when uncertain; invent no physical ratio. A flat
or abstract scene need not model realistic volume. Container fit alone does
not verify object construction.

For a hand holding a tool, preserve contact and working end across required
crops; compare silhouette and detail. If only the caption conveys the action,
repair the visible relation. This example prescribes no style or shot list and
authorizes no constructed documentary evidence.

## Critique: failure signatures and causes

- **Generic or interchangeable image:** the image thesis is missing, selection
  followed mood keywords, or the subject has no relation to the message.
- **Strong single image, weak story:** repeated scale/moment, no establishing or
  detail function, broken chronology, redundant frames, or inconsistent truth
  status undermines the sequence.
- **Crop feels accidental or harmful:** focal subject, gesture, gaze, product,
  contextual evidence or tension is cut because ratios were treated as simple
  masks rather than separate compositions.
- **Text competes or becomes unreadable:** no protected text zone, volatile
  image detail, wrong value structure, or responsive crop moved the background
  under live text. A blanket dark overlay may hide the cause and flatten the
  image.
- **System looks cloned or incoherent:** invariants were confused with fixed
  layouts, or superficial grading was used instead of shared art-direction
  logic.
- **Photograph feels staged unintentionally:** gesture, eye-line, light,
  perspective, environment or object interaction contradicts the declared
  mode and message.
- **Illustration or generated image feels AI-generic:** orb, sparkle, isometric
  city, floating abstract shapes or glossy blob characters may substitute for
  a subject relation; inspect pseudo-detail, construction, ornament and unresolved
  hands/text/objects. Brief from real subject objects, place, process steps,
  working hands, light and scale. Translate references through their mechanism;
  a named style or a legitimate spatial subject is not itself a defect.
- **Photograph reads as stock:** laughing laptop team, handshake, whiteboard,
  skyline drone shot or tidy desk chosen by mood has no image thesis. Capture
  or commission the actual subject in context when permitted. If no real asset
  exists, declare constructed mode and keep the brief subject-bound; never
  fabricate a photographed event. A genuine relevant event image may be familiar.
- **Plausible but ungrounded detail appears:** generation, retouching,
  compositing, reframing or captioning introduced a person, object, text,
  location, event, relationship, scale or result not supported by the source.
- **Output is soft, haloed, banded, posterised or muddy:** effective pixel
  density, sharpening, compression, resampling, gamut, profile, transparency
  or substrate is mismatched to the final use.

Localise each failure, state the likely viewer effect, separate confirmed and
inferred cause, and preserve successful subject, moment, mood, texture,
recognisable details and documentary context.

## Repair and regression

Repair the smallest owning relation. Reselect before attempting to rescue a
wrong image; recrop from the source before enlarging a bad derivative; alter
text placement or choose a stable crop before covering the whole frame; correct
one invariant before normalising every image. In documentary work, remove or
replace unsupported alteration rather than disguising it. In synthetic or
constructed work, correct prohibited inventions and continuity from the brief,
then regenerate or edit only the affected region when the tool can preserve the
rest reliably.

Recheck the full contact sheet, sequence, every target ratio, live text,
responsive focal regions, alt/caption relation, protected subject/context,
truth-mode conditions, participant restrictions, attribution and effective
output quality. A change to the source, crop, treatment or disclosure
invalidates dependent renders and receipts.

## Rules, exceptions and ownership

Binding constraints include supplied facts and source pixels, declared mode,
prohibited inventions, participant conditions, approved assets, required
context, use-specific rights and destination requirements. Conventions such as
clear focal point, complementary shot scale, stable light or consistent
illustration language are mechanisms to test, not a demand for polish or
uniformity. Heuristics such as rule-of-thirds, leading room, eye-level framing,
odd-number groups or one visual style may be broken when the new relation is
clearer and the protected job survives.

Unusual crops, blur, grain, harsh flash, flat perspective, extreme scale,
collage, visible seams, mixed media, discontinuity or repetition can be the
right choice when the gain is named, truth status remains legible, required
content and access survive, and a rendered control does not outperform it.

Imagery owns image job, formal treatment, selection, sequence, crop system and
a local no-invention stop. People/Privacy owns participant agency, documentary
versus synthetic policy, disclosure and privacy. Sources owns factual support.
Asset Rights owns permission and attribution. Production owns delivered-file
proof. This module does not self-certify truth, consent, identity, cultural
safety, legal clearance, originality or provider compliance.

## Proof and claim ceiling

Inspect source/contact sheet beside selected and rejected candidates. Compare
the final sequence as thumbnails and at intended size; render all materially
different ratios with live text, captions and accessibility equivalents.
Inspect originals at enough resolution to distinguish source detail from
artifacts; record edits, crop coordinates or focal intent, model/tool/version
when synthetic, and destination-specific export evidence. Use metadata or
signed provenance only as evidence of association and edit history, never as
proof of truth, permission or quality.

Report `selected from supplied sources`, `crop-inspected`, `sequence-inspected`,
`source-compared`, or `output-rendered` only when performed. Audience response
requires users; documentary integrity requires the governing policy and source
evidence; participant and rights decisions require their owners; physical
reproduction requires the named provider or proof. A convincing render proves
none of those by itself.
