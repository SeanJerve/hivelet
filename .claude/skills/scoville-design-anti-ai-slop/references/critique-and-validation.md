# Critique and validation

Status: `draft`  
Intervention: `focus`  
Sources: `SRC-CRITIQUE-CANON`, `SRC-CRITIQUE-EMPIRICAL`, `SRC-PROFESSIONAL-SCOPE`, `SRC-SOURCE-EVIDENCE`, `SRC-PRODUCTION-PRINT`, `SRC-PRODUCTION-ACCESS`, `SRC-UI-EVALUATION`, `SRC-DESIGN-EVALUATION-EMPIRICAL`, `SRC-PACKAGE-LOCAL-SYNTHESIS`

## Load when

Load for a separate multi-domain causal finding, controlled before/control/after
lifecycle or challenged cross-domain exception. Generic owns specificity
judgment and its counter-move; select it through Core if independently open.
A challenged single-domain exception and its owner-local control comparison
stay with that domain; neither automatically activates this expert.
Ordinary studio self-critique stays in Core. Do not load for
a concept leaf's ordinary candidate/rough comparison or selection, a domain
self-check, or an owner-local proof boundary that does not need a separate
finding/control lifecycle. Do not load for
a single bounded type, colour, data, rights, source, privacy, accessibility, or
production check unless the cross-domain finding lifecycle is also required.
`Critique` never replaces the domain expert that owns the design decision.

## Inputs and evaluation contract

Freeze before inspection:

- target artifact/version and stable identifiers for source and renders;
- brief, audience/task, required content, medium/context, owner, incumbent
  system, constraints, declared intent, approved exceptions, and acceptance
  authority;
- sampled pages, frames, states, locales, data, themes, motion points, target
  sizes, and exclusions;
- available source, render, runtime, user, specialist, production, and
  provenance evidence, each kept distinct;
- preservation set: exact content, facts, dimensions, assets, tokens, strong
  choices, deliberate variation, and unaffected consumers.

If only source or only a screenshot exists, name the missing lane. Do not infer
interaction, hidden states, source ownership, production conditions, permission,
author intent, or audience response from appearance.

For a challenged exception or before/after test, predeclare the question,
control, allowed change, protected dimensions, material render variables,
comparison method, success criterion, and falsifier before outputs are visible.

## Generate and decide the review

1. Follow Core's observation-before-rationale inspection, preserving brief and
   constraints. Record communication, hierarchy, character and task fit before
   reconciling explanation; contradictions use Core's states and exceptions.
2. Inspect intended detail and relevant diagnostic views: thumbnail, sequence,
   narrow/wide state, zoom, print size, alternate theme/locale/data, or motion
   point. Diagnostics reveal different failure classes; they are not audience
   evidence.
3. Localise observations to a stable artifact/page/frame/state/region/element
   anchor. Use `global` or `system` when the cause repeats; never invent a
   bounding box or selector from pixels.
4. Route the cause to concept, content, composition, typography, colour,
   imagery, information, interaction, style, access, rights, culture, source,
   or production. The domain owner decides its rule; this leaf owns the shared
   finding, exception, priority, and validation record.

Keep one working finding record per independent cause, using only applicable
fields. Preserve earlier observations when revising a finding; the full record
is not an extra deliverable unless requested or needed for the agreed handoff:

`ID; artifact/version; scope/location; evidence; observation; authority;
likely effect; verdict; severity; confidence+basis; intent state; parent cause;
smallest repair; preserve; affected consumers; validation; unknowns`.

Allowed verdicts are exactly `defect`, `tradeoff`, `attributed preference`,
`intentional exception`, or `unverifiable`. An experiment is not a verdict.
Keep severity (consequence), confidence (evidence certainty), and priority
(action order) separate. Attribute preferences to a named reviewer or audience;
do not disguise them as laws. Keep overall rank, each candidate's strengths and
local findings distinct: agreement on a winner need not mean agreement on why;
a local flaw is not automatically the reviewer's reason for ranking.
Lower rank, preference or tradeoff alone requires no repair; a direction from
the acceptance authority or documented hard failure still requires the
authorised action.

## Calibrate a cross-domain finding

Fictional example: a brand treatment crops a diagram's endpoint identifiers,
and its owner defends the crop as expressive. The shared finding can say
`endpoint labels absent in the narrow view; relation tracing is no longer
possible from supplied IDs; restore those labels or compare an alternate crop;
preserve the accepted brand device elsewhere; rerender the affected view`.
Diagram semantics owns the protected relation; Brand owns the permitted device;
this leaf coordinates the challenged exception and its proof. A crop that
removes only expendable ornament can remain an intentional exception when the
comparison preserves the task.

By contrast, a caption detached from its own image is an owner-local
composition problem unless another independent concern makes a shared lifecycle
necessary. Do not promote that small repair into a multi-domain review merely
to use this example. The worked finding is original synthesis, not a new
severity score or evidence of audience comprehension.

## Critique: signatures, causes, and false positives

| Failure signature | Cause to test before recommending repair |
| --- | --- |
| Many local symptoms repeat | one rule, token, component, template, content model, asset, or production owner is wrong |
| Artifact is polished but interchangeable | concept lacks subject evidence; style is a familiar palette/type/component bundle rather than a system |
| Finding describes only “weak”, “busy”, or “off” | observation and location are missing; effect, authority, or comparison is inferred from taste |
| High severity rests on uncertain evidence | hidden state, user effect, intent, standard scope, source, or production condition is unverified |
| Deliberate variation is called inconsistency | incumbent baseline, documented intent, convention, and compensating structure were not inspected |
| Screenshot differs from source or baseline | wrong artifact/version/state, font/asset load, crop, renderer/profile, mask, tolerance, or capture failure |
| Before/after seems improved but another state fails | comparison sampled only the edited hero view or changed a material variable |
| Repair removes specificity or hierarchy | a load-bearing strength was not frozen; visible symptom rather than parent cause was edited |

Do not infer a defect from minimal, conventional or incumbent-system styling.
Carry any applicable Generic judgment into this lifecycle without redefining
its ladder or catalog here.

Before calling a signal a design defect, check artifact identity, state, crop,
loading, renderer, baseline approval, heuristic scope, declared intent,
preference, and harness integrity. Preserve original and corrected receipts.
Classify a capture/parser/test failure separately and rerun it; never alter the
baseline, mask, tolerance, or grading rule merely to make a finding disappear.

## Smallest repair and regression control

In `critique`, propose but do not mutate. In `repair`, reproduce the original
failure, freeze the preservation set, then change the smallest authorised parent
cause. Update the canonical rule/token/component/template rather than patching
every symptom. Inspect affected consumers and protected regions, plus other
states exposed to the shared change; rerender or retest under the agreed
comparison contract. Revert or
narrow the change if it damages content, intent, access, identity, system
ownership, or a strong specific cue. Stop once the target cause is resolved and
regressions are absent; a broader redesign needs separate authority.

## Rules and exceptions

Observation is visible evidence; effect and cause may be inference; intent is
`documented`, `inferred`, or `unknown`. Only documented intent can
pre-authorise an exception. For an undocumented deviation, compare the
conventional control and the departure; record gain, cost, protected functional
floor, compensating structure, and falsifier. Do not invent intent after seeing
the result.

An intentional exception survives only when the owner accepts its purpose, the
relevant domain floor holds, the counterstructure is visible, the gain exceeds
the accepted cost, and target evidence does not falsify it. Otherwise classify
the result from evidence without normalising every unusual choice.

## Proof, disagreement, ownership, and claim ceiling

Bind before/control/after to identical non-intervention variables: content,
dimensions, fonts, assets, crops, renderer, profile, state, locale, data, time
and motion point wherever material and unchanged by the authorised repair.
Declare intended changes and dependent effects; do not change unrelated
conditions to manufacture improvement. Declare every mask, tolerance,
normalisation, and exclusion. Proof
must reproduce the original failure, show the corrected target, and inspect
affected consumers plus preserved dimensions. Syntax, metrics, screenshots,
runtime checks, user research, accessibility review, and production proof are
separate evidence lanes.

Use Core's relation binding and final-version evidence matrix for each critical
spatial finding; retain independent finding/control history here.

Record independent reviewers' roles, verdicts, evidence, and rationales before
reconciliation. Keep material disagreement visible; do not average different
constructs into taste, consensus, or a universal score. One reviewer supplies
one reviewer's judgment.

Keep Core's content/execution/preference distinction. Localize observations at
element, whole or system level; separate visible evidence from inferred effect
and cause. Expertise informs judgment, but alone proves no verdict. Missing
localization needs clarification, not automatic classification as taste; keep
unsupported diagnoses unverified and explicit preferences attributed.
Record material overflow, insufficient declared insets,
unintended collision, alignment discrepancy and inconsistent shared type/spacing
even when the concept is preferred. A requested comment cap remains binding;
name the unsampled scope rather than treating omitted findings as approval.

For measurable findings, bind the declared relation and target to an actual
renderer measurement, metric/coordinate space, tolerance and before/after
result. Do not claim ink bounds from glyph-cell boxes or optical balance from
equal coordinates. Missing evidence is unverified, not pass. A successful
recheck covers its named relations only; whole-view judgment remains separate.
Challenge false positives with valid intentional overlaps, optical offsets and
asymmetric controls. Attribute a disputed preference instead of manufacturing
consensus, and correct demonstrably wrong review claims without rewriting the
original independent receipt.

Critique owns causal findings, exception classification, repair priority, and
validation rationale. Domain experts own their design rules; users own task,
comprehension, and preference evidence; qualified specialists own access,
culture, law/rights, data, safety, and production claims. Report the exact
evidence state. Do not certify usability, accessibility, compliance, market
quality, consensus, causal business improvement, or production readiness from
visual critique or one comparison.
