# Scoville Design Anti-AI-Slop

Make visual decisions from the subject and task. Composition foundations live
here; load specialists only for an open decision or a needed method.

## Mode, authority and ownership

| Mode | Work |
| --- | --- |
| Generate | Develop the requested concept and artifact from supplied facts. |
| Critique | Inspect and report read-only; do not redesign to satisfy a generation routine. |
| Repair | Change only the authorised artifact, scope and shared consumers. Inherit valid settled decisions. |

Design owns concept, art direction, visual hierarchy, typography, composition,
spacing, responsive intent, corporate-design/visual-identity definition and critique. Preserve incumbent
systems and protected content. Corporate Identity strategy, culture, behaviour,
naming and verbal identity remain authorised upstream inputs. Never invent
facts, approval, rights, strategy or deliverables.

With optional active-applicable UI, Design defines and judges; that partner
implements framework/component mechanics, states, semantics, accessibility,
responsiveness and runtime proof. Use Coordination for an actual partner exchange
(including Scribe for wording fidelity). Never require or simulate another Skill;
without a partner, handle authorised work directly within the same ownership floors.

## Studio workflow

Working notes are not extra deliverables. Start with
`concern | owner | decision/status | evidence`; expand only material open fields.
Carry source authority, protected constraints and unknowns between phases.
Approval never means verification.

| Stage | Action | Continue when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frame and route | Establish purpose, audience, message, real content, medium, constraints and unknowns; inspect supplied work. Load specialists through the direct index for decisions or methods needed now. | Selected owners match their Load when scope. Typography may lead exploration; later checks alone do not require early specialist loading. |
| Explore or diagnose | Explore subject-grounded ideas and visual treatments at comparable rough fidelity when direction is open. Otherwise locate concerns without reopening settled direction. | Judge the rough's communication and character; choices may be provisional. A useful default or quiet utility direction is valid; a new mechanism is not required. |
| Consolidate | Select the direction, then commit shared spatial values and type roles before extending repetition. | Real-content fit supports the system; retain variants for a visible contribution to hierarchy, reading or overall character. |
| Execute | Build, inspect or repair in authorised scope; mark placeholders and constructed material. | The actual requested artifact is available for its owner's renderer. |
| Inspect | With brief and constraints visible, record the whole's communication, hierarchy and character before consulting design rationale; in self-review defer justification, not knowledge. Then inspect material groups and native details and measure critical relations on that version. | Reconcile rationale with the recorded observation; unsupported contradictions stay disputed, expressive departures use existing exception criteria. Local passes do not clear global findings. Both lanes cover critical relations or missing proof stays explicit. Screenshot creation is not viewing. |
| Resolve and deliver | Identify successful character, classify the failure, repair the parent and rerender affected consumers/neighbours; repeat affected checks and view the final whole again. | Required relations and material whole-view findings are resolved, or report a real authority/evidence/resource limit as partial. Removing useful expression without a supported cause is a regression; never erase a failed or omitted relation. |

After two coherent unsuccessful passes, reassess cause and method; continue while
evidence supports progress, change approach on repeated failure. There is no
one-correction cap, required cosmetic repair or render quota. A clean first
render can pass after applicable checks. Unknown evidence permits safe source
work but not dependent acceptance.

When concept is open, let a verified subject fact visibly shape a decision
(hierarchy, type, colour, image or motion); record fact → decision. If swapping
logo and nouns serves unrelated subjects unchanged, examine the mechanism.
Style is a domain, not a mode. Preserve useful character; novelty is not required.

At consolidation or inspection, if a device adds unrest or its contribution is
unclear, briefly compare the whole without it. Retain it when grouping, rhythm
or expression visibly benefits. Keep unrelated choices fixed; this is a targeted
probe, not a required test of every mark or a preference for fewer elements.

## Composition: decide, bind, inspect

Apply this foundation in every Design task. Steps 1–2 guide exploration with
provisional choices; consolidate before extending repetition. Steps 3–6 govern
inspection and final acceptance, not advance paperwork for each rough variant.
Keep content and binding constraints throughout; address visible failures when
found. Early targeted checks may inform an uncertain direction. Small repairs
scope work to affected regions and shared consumers, without redesigning settled work.

1. **Order before coordinates.** Establish entry, subordinate reads and action.
   Group by meaning; use proximity, similarity, enclosure, connection and
   continuity as potentially conflicting cues. Choose spacing by job:
   within-group, between-group, section, caption and edge. Give negative space
   a purpose (separate, frame, pace, focus); neither empty nor dense is inherently good.
2. **Commit a usable system.** Derive axes, columns, spans, gutters and shared
   values from real content, medium and incumbent systems. Place the longest
   required unit and representative repeated unit before multiplying them.
   Equal roles reuse values; variation needs a role, rhythm or optical reason.
   Set type roles as a coupled size/weight/measure/leading system, not isolated
   sizes. Grid choices can change after a fit check; no universal scale, ratio,
   column count or font-size count applies.
3. **Discover before choosing checks.** Map panels, text, artwork/icons and
   nested/repeated groups. Include the entry, dominant recognizable object and
   dense text group when present and material; inspect a title with any rule or
   eyebrow that frames it. For a motif joining or interrupting a drawn rule, inspect
   both outer junctions and internal gaps as one unit, against intended overlap
   or separation. Observe grouping before measuring. Cross-check
   editable source/layers, including visible use/image instances, not unused
   definitions. Enclosed controls, repeated groups and elements near frame edges
   each need a disposition. Include neighbours and both outside endpoints of
   repeated rows/columns. An empty helper queue does not close this map.
4. **Bind each material relation.** Use
   `object → actual region/anchor | intent + target + basis | owner | metric + view | status`.
   Start unverified. Basis is supplied, incumbent, measured, inferred,
   provisional or unknown; unknown is not zero. Preserve candidates or reasoned
   dispositions rather than selecting them again from memory. A DOM parent,
   growing group box or nearest rectangle is not automatically an enclosure.
   Follow every relevant nested drawn boundary. Enclosure, backdrop and bleed
   are distinct roles. For repeated groups check internal rhythm and both
   external anchors; if no common span applies, retain its visible/source basis.
5. **Match proof to intent.** Declare axis and reference (edge, baseline,
   geometric or optical centre) before measuring. A one-line button/pill/badge
   starts with a centring hypothesis unless its system supports another
   alignment. Text plus icon/arrow is one compound group. A minimum-inset pass
   proves neither centring nor sufficient space. Inspect effective paint,
   strokes, corners and glyph ink; boxes are not optical mass. Choose tolerances
   from scale and precision before results, in one coordinate space. Preserve
   target and local region through repair; a larger container cannot close the
   original failure. If intent changes, retain the old result and explain the
   supported new hypothesis.
6. **Reconcile the final artifact.** Pair actual whole/detail visual observations
   and suitable measured results for every critical relation on the same final
   source/render version. Detail crops must derive from that render; identify
   it in working evidence (use hashes when files are available). Recheck changed
   regions and neighbours, including artwork when text fits. Retain every
   candidate's final disposition; uncovered, stale or unsupported checks remain open.

Load the **Composition methods** index entry only when an open relationship
needs a detailed discovery/measurement procedure or a diagnostic comparison,
and the method's required input is available. Discovery may precede binding;
measurement follows it. Small repair, mark and critique labels alone do not
trigger it. Without suitable inputs, record the missing proof, not a pass.

## Acceptance and repair

| Final visual and suitable metric evidence for a critical relation | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Both pass compatibly on the same version/local relation | pass |
| A failure with no material lane contradiction | fail |
| Pass/fail contradiction or unsupported intentional-exception rationale | disputed |
| Missing, stale, wrong-region or unsupported evidence | unverified |
| Neither lane applies, with a supported reason | not-applicable |

A preferred idea may fail in execution: judge visible type, composition and
image craft, not its explanation. Keep content, execution and preference distinct;
missing feedback is not agreement and independent disagreement stays visible.

Diagnose in order: content/requirement → semantic order → container/measure →
grid/span → scale/density → crop/source → local optical correction. Stop at the
first supported cause; repair its canonical owner and inspect consumers. Do not conceal
failures through clipping, blanket centring, more whitespace, smaller type or
one-column stacking. Revert or narrow regressions. Cause may be confirmed,
inferred or unknown.

At acceptance, an exception needs supported intent/gain, protected communication/access floors,
counterstructure, cost and a falsifying observation. Compare a fair conventional
control when challenged; keep non-intervention variables constant, allowing the
repair's declared dependent effects. A plausible story after the fact is not
evidence. Never reclassify user-rejected overflow as intentional bleed. Source,
rights, consent and production constraints cannot become aesthetic exceptions.
Ordinary local exceptions stay with their specialist; Critique handles a separate
multi-domain lifecycle or challenged cross-domain exception.

## Route and read only what applies

Use direct index links. General composition foundations never require another
module. Specialists retain their geometry: packaging faces, mark contours,
chart encodings, map topology, responsive pressure and fixed-media structure.
Independent concerns still route: label appearance does not alone select
Information, but a contradicted value-to-position relation does; Wayfinding
does not settle map geometry; Mark does not reopen Brand/Concept unless open.
Brand also owns an authored icon-family or stroke system outside identity work.
Revisit routing when a new concern appears; no fixed module-count/token gate.

Verify received coverage before applying a selected source: numbered lines,
total, received and missing intervals. Recover missing or cut-through lines,
including intact adjacent lines; reconcile each file separately. Keep conflicting
returns and source order. An end marker is not proof of a complete middle.
Use the optional [source reader](scripts/read-source.py) or an equivalent.
Its header contains recovery details when transport fails. If coverage cannot
be recovered, carry the dependent concern unverified. Headings do not unload
context; split phases only for actual task/capacity needs. User/provider limits
and authorisation remain binding.

## Proof ceiling

Distinguish binding constraints, evidence-bounded rules, conventions, heuristics,
preferences and exceptions. Numeric advice needs its population, language/script,
medium, task and override basis; one study or starting value is no universal law.
Render proof covers inspected appearance only. It requires a host capability
through which the agent actually views the rendered artifact or its image, such
as an image-viewing tool, a screenshot that the agent can open, or a supplied
render. Creating a screenshot file, reading its bytes or metadata, or inspecting
source alone is not viewing. Without such a capability, record every visual-lane
result as unverified and state that limit. Syntax, runtime, accessibility,
user research, rights, provider and physical acceptance are separate lanes.
Check current standards, law, licences and living-community claims when required;
if unavailable, mark unverified and stop at that authority boundary.


## Terms

- **Module / expert / leaf:** one linked reference and its domain role, not a subagent.
- **Owner:** the person/domain authorised to change a field's decision or status.
- **Observer:** reports attributed effects without changing another owner's decision.
- **Falsifier:** the observation that would overturn a judgment or exception.
- **Control:** a fair comparison preserving unrelated conditions.
- **Claim ceiling:** the strongest claim the observed evidence actually supports.

## Direct expert index

`draft` is development status, not admission or a quality verdict. Metadata
`evidence` values are identifiers for external development receipts, not files
included in this installed package. An empty list means none is referenced;
a nonempty list may reference failures or limited results, not a pass or general
qualification. Without the external record, its outcome is unverified. Do not
fetch receipts for ordinary use. The package does not establish measured benefit
over an unaided model. Route by open concern, not status.
