## Preamble

This errata sheet accompanies the Module 01 refinement submission. Every correction listed below was identified by the group itself, during a systematic line-by-line architecture review in which each documentary claim was re-verified against the live repository — the schema file, the seeded data, the backend source tree, and the frontend build configuration — rather than against earlier drafts of our own documents.

We publish corrected artifacts and this sheet together rather than silently reissuing the documents. Where a submitted statement described a design intention as though it were an implemented fact, we say so plainly and reclassify it as Phase 2 or Phase 3 work. Where two of our documents disagreed, we name the canonical source and retire the other reading. Where a performance number could not be reproduced from a measurement, we withdraw it.

Twenty-one items follow. Each cites the exact file and line as submitted, the fact established by inspection, and the correction now in force. Two of them, E-20 and E-21, additionally rest on decisions the owner confirmed on 2026-09-13, after the Module 01 submission was made; those two supersede submitted wording rather than correct an error of transcription. Line numbers refer to the files as submitted for Module 01.

## Namespace Note (a disambiguation the panel should hold, not an error)

Two numbering schemes were circulating in our working material. `docs/02_BUSINESS_RULES.md` (BR-001 … BR-049) is the **one canonical BR namespace**, and every citation in the refined artifacts resolves against it. The seven "pillars" previously carried with `BR-` identifiers in `docs/claude_pipeline/CLAUDE_PIPELINE.md` are architectural pillars, not business rules. They are renumbered **ARCH-001 … ARCH-007** and map onto canonical business rules as follows.

| Pillar | Name | Canonical business rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ARCH-001 | Room-Centric Tenancy | BR-001, BR-002, BR-003, BR-032 |
| ARCH-002 | Dynamic Utility Water | BR-014, BR-034, BR-036, BR-040 |
| ARCH-003 | 50% Share Ledger Parity | BR-035 |
| ARCH-004 | Rate Change History | BR-003 [^arch004] |
| ARCH-005 | Hybrid Gateway Adapter | BR-016, BR-017 |
| ARCH-006 | Backend Security Boundary | BR-006, BR-048 |
| ARCH-007 | Immutable Audit Trail | BR-018, BR-028 |

A pillar identifier is never written with a `BR-` prefix in the refined artifacts.

[^arch004]: ARCH-004 was carried in earlier drafts against canonical **BR-009**, whose registered title at `docs/02_BUSINESS_RULES.md:46` is *Inquiry Conversion*, which was never its subject. The automated rate-adjustment feature that the pillar was built around is withdrawn from scope in full (see **E-20**): room rates are set manually by the administrator, and every change is written to `room_price_history` with its effective date and the administrator who made it. The pillar is therefore renamed **Rate Change History** and anchored to canonical **BR-003** *Historical Preservation* (`docs/02_BUSINESS_RULES.md:13`), the rule it genuinely implements.
