Hivelet: your tenant portal

hivelet.vercel.app

1. First sign-in / Unang pag-sign in

  1. Open hivelet.vercel.app, tap Sign in.
  2. Type your login ID (for example HV-48213) and the starting password on your slip.
  3. Add your own email and mobile number, and set your own password: type the starting one again, then the new one twice. At least 10 characters, a letter and a number.

I-type ang login ID at ang starting password sa slip, saka ilagay ang sariling email, numero at password (10+ character, may letra at numero).

2. What you will see / Makikita ninyo

  • Overview: your unit, rent and Amount due. It counts only payments the landlady has recorded.
  • Payments and billing: rent plus water at ₱200 per occupant a month, and Your rent, month by month: each month Paid, Due or Not entered (not yet entered by the landlady, not the same as owing).
  • My details: keep your number and emergency contact right.

Ang "Amount due" ay batay lang sa mga bayad na naitala na ng landlady.

3. Report a repair / Mag-report ng sira

  1. Repairs > say what needs fixing and How urgent.
  2. Add a photo if you can (a normal phone photo is fine).
  3. Send request. Follow it and add notes there; you are told when it is done.

4. Put it on your home screen

  • Android (Chrome): ⋮ > Install app.
  • iPhone (Safari): Share > Add to Home Screen.

No signal? It still opens, says "No connection" and shows your bill as last loaded; nothing is sent or paid until the signal is back.

Paying: pay the landlady in person as usual. Pay with GCash does not charge real money yet. Sa landlady pa rin po magbayad nang personal.
Forgot your password? Ask the landlady; she can give you a new starting password. Five wrong tries lock it for 15 minutes. Nakalimutan? Lumapit sa landlady.

Keep your password to yourself. Sign out on a phone that is not yours (your initials, top right > Sign out). Fe Galang Da Silva Boarding House.
