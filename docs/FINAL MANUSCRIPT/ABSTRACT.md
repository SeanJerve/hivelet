Small boarding houses often run on spreadsheets, paper receipts and messages that are not connected
to one another, which the general-purpose property management systems on the market do not fit.
This study designed, developed and evaluated Hivelet, a web-based apartment management system
delivered as a Progressive Web Application, for the Fe Galang Da Silva Boarding House, a 33-unit
property in Legazpi City. The owner's existing practices were analyzed, and her own records,
937 income rows and 1,327 expense allocations, were transferred into the system, which exposed
problems the spreadsheet had hidden. The system was built in three Agile iterations with Vue,
Express and PostgreSQL, and provides tenant and room management, inquiries about units, financial
tracking with optional GCash payment through Adyen, maintenance requests with notifications,
role-based access control, and installation on any phone or computer. It was pilot tested at the
unit and integration, system, and acceptance levels, and 82 of the 83 tests executed passed (98.8
per cent), with no critical defect. Three tenants completed every task they attempted, 31 of 34
attempts without help. The system was then evaluated on the eight characteristics of the ISO/IEC
25010:2011 quality model, following the ISO/IEC 25040 evaluation process, on two kinds of evidence:
measures from the tests, and a survey of the owner, three tenants, a prospective tenant and a
technical evaluator. The respondents rated it 4.53 overall, Very High Quality, with every
characteristic at least High Quality; Portability rated highest and Maintainability and Usability
lowest, and the measurements agreed with the ratings on every characteristic but Usability. The
results show that an integrated system can be fitted to a small, cash-based property without
changing how its owner keeps her accounts. The study met its general objective: Hivelet was
designed, developed and evaluated as a web-based apartment management system that integrates the
boarding house's administrative workflow, communication and operational control.
