Small boarding houses often run on spreadsheets, paper receipts and messages that are not connected,
and the general-purpose property management systems on the market do not fit them. This study
designed, developed and evaluated Hivelet, a web-based apartment management system delivered as a
Progressive Web Application, for the Fe Galang Da Silva Boarding House, a 33-unit property in Legazpi
City. The boarding house's existing practices were analyzed, and transferring its records, 937 income
rows and 1,327 expense allocations, into the system exposed problems the spreadsheet had hidden. The
system was developed in two Agile iterations with Vue, Express and PostgreSQL. It provides tenant
and room management, a public list of units with inquiries the landlady answers, financial tracking
with optional GCash payment through Adyen, repair requests with notifications, role-based access
control, and installation on a phone or computer without an app store. The GCash payment works end to
end in Adyen's test environment and takes no real money. It is not live, because going live needs the
owner's merchant onboarding documents. In pilot testing at the unit and integration, system, and
acceptance levels, 82 of the 83 tests executed passed (98.8 percent), with no critical defect. Three
tenants completed every task they attempted, 31 of 34 attempts without help, and on the redesigned
system ten tenants and four prospective tenants completed every task. The system was evaluated on
the eight characteristics of ISO/IEC 25010:2011 by the process of ISO/IEC 25040, using measures from
the tests and a survey. The landlady, ten tenants and three prospective tenants rated the redesigned system 4.84 overall, Very High Quality, with every characteristic Very High Quality. Three technical evaluators rated it 4.90, also with every characteristic Very High Quality.
The measurements supported the ratings on six characteristics and were less favorable on Security
and Maintainability. The results show that an integrated system can be fitted to a small, cash-based
property while keeping its landlady's own report layout, invoice numbers and cash payments. The study met its general objective: Hivelet
was designed, developed and evaluated as a web-based apartment management system that improves
administrative workflow integration, communication and operational control at the boarding house.
