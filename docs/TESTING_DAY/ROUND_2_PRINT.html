Testing round 2: read this first

Print: this page once. The consent form (page 2) one per person. The tenant task cards (page 3) once for the observer. The tenant observation sheet (page 4) one per tenant. The prospect page (page 5) one per prospective tenant. Page 6 is the list of what to send back.

Who

  • 5 to 7 tenants who were not among the three on 30 September. Mix them: different units and clusters, someone who pays cash, someone less used to apps, Android and iPhone.
  • 1 or 2 prospective tenants: people who do not live there (a friend, a student looking for a room).
  • Nobody from the team tests or answers the survey.

Tester codes: continue from round 1

Tenants are T4, T5, T6 ... (T1 to T3 were used on 30 September). Prospects are PR2, PR3. Use the code on every sheet, never the name. Only the consent form carries the name.

Each session, in this order

  1. Consent form first. Signed before anything else. Fill in Research team member present yourself.
  2. Show the same short Hivelet video as on 30 September.
  3. Read each task card aloud (English, then Filipino). Write start time, end time, success, wrong taps, and what they say.
  4. Help only when they are stuck, and write the level: 1 = a prompt ("what would you try?"), 2 = a hint where to look, 3 = shown how.
  5. The survey straight away, on the same Google Form as 30 September (do not make a new one). Show the QR, step away while they answer.

Rules

  • The system is live. A test repair request has a title starting TEST. Inquiries write TEST in the message.
  • Nobody completes a GCash payment. Open it, look, close it.
  • Nobody types a password for someone else or photographs a password or slip.
  • At most two inquiries from the same wifi in 15 minutes, then use mobile data.
  • Tenants on their first sign-in need their sign-in slip from the landlady (login ID and temporary password). Ask her for the slips beforehand.
  • Photos or screen recordings only if the consent form says yes. Write what happened, not what should have happened.

Run all of them by about 8 November, so the paper can be updated and sent to the adviser before the defense.
