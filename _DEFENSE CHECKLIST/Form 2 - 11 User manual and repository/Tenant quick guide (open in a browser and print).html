Hivelet: your tenant portal

hivelet.vercel.app

1. First sign-in / Unang pag-sign in

  1. Open hivelet.vercel.app, tap Sign in.
  2. Type your login ID (for example HV-48213) and the starting password on your slip.
  3. Add your own mobile number, and set your own password: type the starting one again, then the new one twice. At least 10 characters, a letter and a number.

I-type ang login ID at ang starting password sa slip, saka ilagay ang sariling numero at password (10+ character, may letra at numero).

2. What you will see / Makikita ninyo

  • Overview: your unit and deposit, your payments, your monthly charge and the landlady's number. Amount due shows while something is to pay, counting only payments the landlady has recorded.
  • Payments and billing: the bill to pay (rent plus water at ₱200 per occupant a month), then History and Record. Not recorded means not yet recorded by the landlady, not owing.
  • My details: your number, emergency contact and Change password.

Ang "Amount due" ay batay lang sa mga bayad na naitala na ng landlady.

3. Report a repair / Mag-report ng sira

  1. Repairs (or Request a repair on your Overview) > What needs fixing, Category, How urgent and Details.
  2. Add a photo if you can (a normal phone photo is fine).
  3. Send request. Tap it, then Progress and notes, to follow it through Submitted, In progress and Done and add notes. You are told when it is done.

4. Put it on your home screen

  • Your initials, top right > Add to home screen.
  • Or Android (Chrome): ⋮ > Install app. iPhone (Safari): Share > Add to Home Screen.

Pull down from the top of a page to reload it.

No signal? It still opens, says "No connection" and shows your bill, payments, requests and notifications as last loaded. Nothing is sent or paid until the signal is back.

Pay with GCash: optional online payment, not live yet. It works in Adyen's test environment and takes no real money. Pay the landlady in person. Sa landlady pa rin po magbayad nang personal.
Forgot your password? Ask the landlady. She can give you a new starting password. Five wrong passwords lock the account for 15 minutes, then it opens again by itself. Nakalimutan? Lumapit sa landlady.

Keep your password to yourself. Sign out on a phone that is not yours (your initials, top right > Sign out). Fe Galang Da Silva Boarding House.
